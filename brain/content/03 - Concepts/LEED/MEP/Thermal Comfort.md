# Thermal Comfort

## Definition

Thermal comfort concerns the combined effects of indoor temperature, thermal radiation, humidity, air speed, occupant activity, and clothing. Standard 55-2023 states that these factors are to be considered together rather than as isolated setpoints. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 55 p. 4]]

## Why It Matters

Current BD+C includes a Thermal Environment option within Occupant Experience, connecting thermal conditions with seasonal change, occupant transitions, different activity levels, and opportunities for adjustment. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 164|BD+C p. 164]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 166|BD+C p. 166]]

Current O+M evaluates occupant experience and satisfaction with the indoor environment through a survey pathway, providing an operational feedback boundary rather than a separate design-compliance path in the cited page. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 078|O+M p. 78]]

## Authority and Version Scope

- **BD+C:** Verified against the November 2025 published rating system, which explicitly references Standard 55-2023 with errata. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 166|BD+C p. 166]]
- **ID+C:** Source-gated. The April 2025 launch guide describes a parallel thermal-environment option, but it is guidance rather than current published authority. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 324|ID+C guide p. 324]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 326|ID+C guide p. 326]]
- **O+M:** The cited current rating-system page verifies an occupant-experience survey boundary; it does not establish a separate Standard 55 design option. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 078|O+M p. 78]]
- **Technical standard:** The restricted local document is confirmed as ANSI/ASHRAE Standard 55-2023, superseding the 2020 edition. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 001|ASHRAE 55 p. 1]]

## LEED v5 Requirements

### BD+C

The Thermal Environment option requires occupied spaces to meet Standard 55-2023 with errata and asks the design to investigate project thermal conditions, including seasonal change, overcooling, transitions between environments, and different occupant activities. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 166|BD+C p. 166]]

The option sits within the broader Occupant Experience credit and is one selectable pathway rather than a standalone prerequisite. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 164|BD+C p. 164]]

### ID+C

**Source-gated:** the April 2025 launch guide presents a Commercial Interiors Thermal Environment option that references Standard 55-2023 and similar occupant and seasonal considerations. It is not promoted to a current published ID+C requirement. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 324|ID+C guide p. 324]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 326|ID+C guide p. 326]]

### O+M

The current O+M Occupant Experience Performance credit uses an occupant survey to assess satisfaction with the indoor environment during the reporting period. The cited page does not isolate thermal comfort as a separate compliance calculation. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 078|O+M p. 78]]

## MEP and Piping Application

> [!note] Vault-authored engineering interpretation
> The applications below are HVAC design and verification practices, not additional LEED requirements.

- Translate occupancy, activity, envelope exposure, solar loads, and space use into thermal zones before selecting terminals and controls. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 55 p. 4]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 166|BD+C p. 166]]
- Coordinate air temperature, radiant effects, humidity, and air movement rather than treating thermostat setpoint as the complete comfort outcome. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 55 p. 4]]
- Provide appropriate occupant adjustment or differentiated zones where project use and the selected LEED pathway support them. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 166|BD+C p. 166]]
- Verify sensors, sequences, balancing, and representative operating conditions through commissioning and post-occupancy feedback. Standard 55 identifies design documentation and measurement of existing environments as distinct verification activities. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 55 p. 4]]

## Engineering Workflow

1. Define occupied spaces, tasks, schedules, clothing and activity assumptions, and relevant environmental factors. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 55 p. 4]]
2. Develop zoning, load, terminal-unit, air-distribution, and control strategies responsive to those conditions. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 166|BD+C p. 166]]
3. Document the Standard 55 method and the project assumptions used to evaluate occupied spaces. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 55 p. 4]]
4. Test sensors, control responses, and representative environmental conditions during commissioning. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 55 p. 4]]
5. Use occupant feedback as operational evidence and investigate patterns by zone, time, and condition before changing controls. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 078|O+M p. 78]]

## Documentation and Evidence

- **BD+C requirement evidence:** Standard 55 compliance documentation and the project explanation of seasonal, transitional, and activity-related thermal conditions. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 166|BD+C p. 166]]
- **O+M evidence:** occupant survey results and reporting-period records under the selected current O+M pathway. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 078|O+M p. 78]]
- **Engineering evidence:** zoning diagrams, load assumptions, control narratives, sensor calibration, trend reviews, balancing records, and issue logs can support traceability without becoming new LEED requirements. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 55 p. 4]]

## Technical Standards

- ANSI/ASHRAE Standard 55-2023 is the confirmed local edition and the edition explicitly named by current BD+C. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 001|ASHRAE 55 p. 1]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 166|BD+C p. 166]]
- Its scope combines environmental and personal factors and distinguishes design compliance from measurement and evaluation of existing environments. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 55 p. 4]]
- This note does not reproduce comfort limits, equations, or tables from the restricted standard.
