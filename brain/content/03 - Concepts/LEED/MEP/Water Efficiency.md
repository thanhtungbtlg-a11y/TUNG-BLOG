# Water Efficiency

## Definition

Water efficiency is the coordinated reduction, measurement, and management of potable and alternative water used by a building and its grounds. In current LEED v5 sources, BD+C addresses design-stage metering and minimum and enhanced efficiency, while O+M addresses whole-project metering and measured operational performance. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 061|BD+C p. 61]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 062|BD+C p. 62]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 032|O+M p. 32]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 033|O+M p. 33]]

## Why It Matters

The cited LEED sources connect water efficiency with conserving potable water and reducing the energy and emissions associated with water treatment and distribution. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 062|BD+C p. 62]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 033|O+M p. 33]]

## LEED v5 Requirements

### BD+C

Water Metering and Reporting requires permanent measurement of total potable and alternative water supplied to the building and associated grounds, separate measurement by source type, accessible data, and ongoing reporting commitments. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 061|BD+C p. 61]]

Minimum Water Efficiency spans fixtures and fittings, equipment and process water, and outdoor water use, with project-scope distinctions for Core and Shell. This note does not reproduce fixture values, reduction percentages, or calculation rules. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 062|BD+C p. 62]]

Water Metering and Leak Detection expands measurement to applicable subsystems, while Enhanced Water Efficiency offers whole-project, indoor, outdoor, process-water, cooling, and reuse pathways. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 066|BD+C p. 66]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 069|BD+C p. 69]]

The captured BD+C addenda correct guide explanations and documentation for metering and water-efficiency paths; they do not replace the published rating-system pages. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 003|BD+C addenda p. 3]]

### ID+C

**Source-gated:** the April 2025 launch guide describes tenant-space fixture and equipment efficiency, metering and leak detection, and enhanced indoor and process-water pathways. These remain guidance-only until a current published ID+C rating system is validated. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 082|ID+C guide p. 82]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 089|ID+C guide p. 89]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 095|ID+C guide p. 95]]

The guide identifies cooling towers and other process-water uses as distinct optimization areas and discusses the relationship among cycles, treatment, blowdown, maintenance, and conservation. No numeric rule is carried into this note. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 102|ID+C guide p. 102]]

### O+M

Water Metering and Reporting requires whole-project measurement and reporting of potable and alternative water sources over the reporting period, with data access and annual sharing commitments. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 032|O+M p. 32]]

Water Efficiency Performance evaluates total potable water consumption over the reporting period against the applicable pathway. This note intentionally omits the source's performance tables and thresholds. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 033|O+M p. 33]]

Advanced Water Metering identifies irrigation, indoor fixtures, cooling towers, domestic hot water, reclaimed water, and other process water as possible subsystem boundaries and requires recurring recording and trend analysis. This note does not reproduce subsystem percentages. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 035|O+M p. 35]]

## MEP and Piping Application

> [!note] Vault-authored engineering interpretation
> The applications below translate the cited categories into coordination practices; they are not additional LEED requirements.

- Build a project water balance that separates potable, alternative, indoor, irrigation, domestic-hot-water, cooling-tower, and other process-water boundaries. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 061|BD+C p. 61]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 069|BD+C p. 69]]
- Coordinate fixture schedules, domestic-water sizing, irrigation demand, cooling-tower makeup and blowdown, process loads, and meter locations before construction documents are finalized. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 062|BD+C p. 62]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 066|BD+C p. 66]]
- Treat design-stage efficiency and operational performance as different evidence problems: one defines installed scope and modeled or prescriptive performance, while the other depends on measured use over time. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 062|BD+C p. 62]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 033|O+M p. 33]]
- Use subsystem meters and leak alarms to support diagnosis, but retain whole-project source metering as a separate reporting boundary. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 061|BD+C p. 61]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 066|BD+C p. 66]]

## Engineering Workflow

1. Confirm rating system, project boundary, owner and tenant scope, water sources, and reporting period. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 061|BD+C p. 61]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 032|O+M p. 32]]
2. Inventory indoor fixtures, appliances, irrigation, domestic-hot-water systems, cooling towers, and other process-water uses. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 062|BD+C p. 62]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 035|O+M p. 35]]
3. Select efficiency and alternative-water strategies without mixing design calculations with operational performance claims. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 069|BD+C p. 69]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 033|O+M p. 33]]
4. Coordinate meter, submeter, sensor, controls, access, and data responsibilities. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 066|BD+C p. 66]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 035|O+M p. 35]]
5. Review measured trends, reconcile source and subsystem data, and document corrective action or conservation opportunities. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 032|O+M p. 32]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 035|O+M p. 35]]

## Documentation and Evidence

- **BD+C design evidence:** water-source and meter diagrams, fixture and equipment schedules, irrigation and process-water scope, selected-path calculations, and required product documentation. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 061|BD+C p. 61]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 062|BD+C p. 62]]
- **O+M operating evidence:** whole-project meter records, source separation, reporting-period data, subsystem trends where pursued, and calibration records where applicable. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 032|O+M p. 32]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 035|O+M p. 35]]
- **Engineering evidence:** water-balance diagrams, meter schedules, leak-response procedures, trend reviews, and reconciliation logs can make performance claims auditable. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 066|BD+C p. 66]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 035|O+M p. 35]]
