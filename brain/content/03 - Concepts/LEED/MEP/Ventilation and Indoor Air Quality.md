# Ventilation and Indoor Air Quality

## Definition

Ventilation and indoor air quality combine outdoor-air delivery with air-cleaning, contaminant control, system access, operation, and verification. The confirmed local Standard 62.1-2022 addresses ventilation and air-cleaning design, installation, commissioning, and O&M, while LEED v5 adds rating-system-specific design or operating provisions. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-62-1-2022-1/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 62.1 p. 4]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 157|BD+C p. 157]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 068|O+M p. 68]]

## Why It Matters

The cited LEED v5 requirements are intended to support occupant health by investigating outdoor-air conditions, designing ventilation and filtration, and verifying delivered outdoor air and system operation. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 157|BD+C p. 157]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 068|O+M p. 68]]

## LEED v5 Requirements

### BD+C

Fundamental Air Quality requires an outdoor-air-quality investigation and ventilation and filtration design under specified sections of Standard 62.1-2022. The rating-system page permits the ventilation-rate, IAQ, natural-ventilation, or combined procedures and adds filtration provisions. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 157|BD+C p. 157]]

Enhanced Air Quality provides alternatives for increased ventilation or enhanced IAQ design, and Air Quality Testing and Monitoring addresses preoccupancy testing or monitoring pathways. This note does not reproduce their numeric criteria or tables. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 162|BD+C p. 162]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 178|BD+C p. 178]]

### ID+C

**Source-gated:** the April 2025 launch guide describes outdoor-air investigation, Standard 62.1-2022 ventilation and filtration design, and enhanced-air-quality options for Commercial Interiors. These remain guidance-level until a current published ID+C rating system is validated. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 308|ID+C guide p. 308]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 319|ID+C guide p. 319]]

### O+M

Verification of Ventilation and Filtration requires operating and maintenance information, an outdoor-air investigation, calculation of required outdoor air, and measurement of delivered outdoor air for mechanically ventilated spaces. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 068|O+M p. 68]]

Ventilation Performance uses the prerequisite measurements to compare occupied-space outdoor-air delivery with referenced Standard 62.1 procedures. The captured addendum clarifies placement and wording related to increased outdoor air or opening sizes. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 077|O+M p. 77]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-addenda-table-all-02-20-2026/pages/Page 004|O+M addenda p. 4]]

IAQ Performance provides continuous-monitoring and one-time-testing pathways; the captured addenda revise monitor qualification wording and related guidance. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 073|O+M p. 73]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-addenda-table-all-02-20-2026/pages/Page 003|O+M addenda p. 3]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-addenda-table-all-02-20-2026/pages/Page 004|O+M addenda p. 4]]

## MEP and Piping Application

> [!note] Vault-authored engineering interpretation
> The applications below are coordination practices, not additional LEED requirements.

- Map outdoor-air intakes, exhausts, contaminant sources, air-cleaning devices, sensors, and occupied zones before finalizing airflow and control sequences. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 157|BD+C p. 157]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-62-1-2022-1/pages/Page 017|ASHRAE 62.1 p. 17]]
- Provide maintainable access to ventilation equipment, filters, controllers, sensors, coils, drain pans, and humidifiers where those components require routine inspection or service. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-62-1-2022-1/pages/Page 017|ASHRAE 62.1 p. 17]]
- Coordinate outdoor-air measurement, testing and balancing, calibration, BMS points, and O&M procedures so design intent can be checked during operation. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 068|O+M p. 68]]
- Keep normal ventilation design separate from episodic wildfire-smoke or other resilience modes; the current rating systems place episodic outdoor-event modes in Resilient Spaces, and detailed methods remain source-gated pending Guideline 44-2024. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 176|BD+C p. 176]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 082|O+M p. 82]]

## Engineering Workflow

1. Investigate regional and local outdoor-air conditions and identify project-specific contaminant sources. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 157|BD+C p. 157]]
2. Select and document the applicable Standard 62.1 procedure, zone assumptions, outdoor-air paths, filtration, and exhaust strategy. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-62-1-2022-1/pages/Page 017|ASHRAE 62.1 p. 17]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-62-1-2022-1/pages/Page 018|ASHRAE 62.1 p. 18]]
3. Coordinate access, controls, sensors, TAB points, and commissioning checks. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-62-1-2022-1/pages/Page 017|ASHRAE 62.1 p. 17]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 068|O+M p. 68]]
4. During operation, compare measured delivery with the applicable requirement and record corrective action where performance is deficient. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 068|O+M p. 68]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 069|O+M p. 69]]
5. Use the selected monitoring or testing pathway without substituting sensor data for required ventilation verification. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 073|O+M p. 73]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 077|O+M p. 77]]

## Documentation and Evidence

- **Design evidence:** outdoor-air investigation, ventilation calculations, filtration selection, plans, schedules, sequences, and procedure selection. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 157|BD+C p. 157]]
- **Operating evidence:** O&M plan, site survey, airflow measurements, calibration information, filtration records, and documented correction of deficiencies. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 068|O+M p. 68]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 069|O+M p. 69]]
- **Performance evidence:** monitoring or testing records must follow the selected LEED pathway and applicable addenda. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 073|O+M p. 73]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-addenda-table-all-02-20-2026/pages/Page 003|O+M addenda p. 3]]
