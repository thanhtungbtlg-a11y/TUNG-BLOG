# Integrative Design Process

## Definition

Integrative design is an early, facilitated, interdisciplinary process that uses building-system relationships to set measurable goals and coordinate decisions from predesign into occupancy. In LEED v5 BD+C, this general principle is also expressed as a formal credit with defined team, charrette, and goal-setting requirements. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 019|BD+C p. 19]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/foundations-of-leed-1-2023/pages/Page 004|Foundations p. 4]]

## Why It Matters

The process is intended to produce high-performance, cost-effective, cross-functional outcomes and to help teams address decarbonization, quality of life, and ecological conservation and restoration as connected objectives. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 019|BD+C p. 19]]

The reference-guide model extends the work through discovery, design and construction, and occupancy, operations, and performance feedback, so decisions can be tested against outcomes rather than treated as isolated checklist items. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773644-leed-v5-bd-c-reference-guide-launch-edition/pages/Page 050|BD+C guide p. 50]]

## LEED v5 Requirements

### BD+C

IPc1 is a one-point New Construction and Core and Shell credit. From predesign through early occupancy, the team must pursue system synergies through an integrated interdisciplinary team with organized facilitation, a predesign or early-design charrette involving the owner or representative and at least four key perspectives, and measurable goals for the LEED v5 impact areas incorporated into the owner's project requirements. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 019|BD+C p. 19]]

### ID+C

The April 2025 launch guide presents a one-point Commercial Interiors credit with two alternatives: follow an integrative design process or locate in a LEED-certified building. Its integrative path includes an interdisciplinary team, a charrette with the owner or representative and at least four perspectives, and measurable impact-area goals in the owner's project requirements. These are launch-guide provisions, not verified current ID+C requirements in this vault. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 046|ID+C guide p. 46]]

### O+M

No same-titled current O+M credit is listed in the captured rating system. The closest current operational mechanisms require an assessment of practices, baselines and goals where appropriate, a sustainable operations policy, and a current facilities requirements and O+M plan containing operating information for mechanical and electrical systems. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 002|O+M p. 2]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 016|O+M p. 16]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 018|O+M p. 18]]

## MEP and Piping Application

> [!note] Vault-authored engineering interpretation
> The applications below translate the sourced integrative-process principle into MEP coordination practice. They are not additional LEED requirements.

- Coordinate envelope assumptions, HVAC loads and zoning, water demand and heat recovery, electrical capacity, controls, commissioning, and operating sequences before individual systems are fixed. This applies the source's direction to investigate interdependencies and avoid solving one system at the expense of another. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 037|LEED v4 foundation p. 37]]
- Use the owner's project requirements as the shared place to translate impact-area goals into performance targets that the MEP design and later commissioning work can test. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 019|BD+C p. 19]]
- Include facility operators early enough to challenge access, maintainability, metering, controls, and handover assumptions before construction documents make changes expensive. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773644-leed-v5-bd-c-reference-guide-launch-edition/pages/Page 050|BD+C guide p. 50]]

## Engineering Workflow

1. **Discovery:** assemble the decision-makers, identify system interactions, establish measurable goals, and record them in the owner's project requirements before schematic design. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 019|BD+C p. 19]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773644-leed-v5-bd-c-reference-guide-launch-edition/pages/Page 050|BD+C guide p. 50]]
2. **Option testing:** compare coordinated alternatives across energy, water, controls, constructability, commissioning, and operations; the cross-system comparison is an engineering interpretation of the documented discovery process. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773644-leed-v5-bd-c-reference-guide-launch-edition/pages/Page 050|BD+C guide p. 50]]
3. **Design and construction:** carry agreed interactions into design criteria, sequences, specifications, and coordination reviews rather than allowing disciplines to optimize in isolation. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 047|ID+C guide p. 47]]
4. **Occupancy and feedback:** prepare operating teams to measure results, compare performance with targets, and identify corrective action. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773644-leed-v5-bd-c-reference-guide-launch-edition/pages/Page 050|BD+C guide p. 50]]

## Documentation and Evidence

- **Required in the cited BD+C credit:** evidence must support the integrated team, facilitated charrette, measurable impact-area goals, and incorporation of those goals into the owner's project requirements. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 019|BD+C p. 19]]
- **Possible project records, not asserted as universal LEED deliverables:** team roster, charrette agenda and decisions, system-option matrix, updated owner's project requirements, coordinated basis narratives, and an action log. These examples implement the source's iterative workshop and performance-feedback model. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773644-leed-v5-bd-c-reference-guide-launch-edition/pages/Page 050|BD+C guide p. 50]]
- **O+M evidence:** current operations policy and facilities planning records are separate O+M requirements and should not be mislabeled as proof of the BD+C credit. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 016|O+M p. 16]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 018|O+M p. 18]]

## Technical Standards

No technical standard is used to define this credit in the cited current BD+C requirement. Standards may govern technical decisions developed through the process, but their applicability must be established by the relevant LEED prerequisite, credit, code, or project requirement rather than inferred from the Integrative Design Process title. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 019|BD+C p. 19]]

## Version and Interpretation Notes

- LEED v4 describes integrative process as a general systems method with discovery, implementation, and feedback phases; LEED v5 BD+C retains that principle and adds a formal credit with explicit participation and goal-setting provisions. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 037|LEED v4 foundation p. 37]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 019|BD+C p. 19]]
- The official high-level summary places Integrative Design Process in the new Integrative Process, Planning, and Assessments category, but it is a comparison aid rather than the source of detailed requirements. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-bdc-v4-v5-summary-of-changes/pages/Page 002|Summary p. 2]]
- ID+C must be rechecked when the current published rating system is locally validated; the April 2025 launch guide cannot close that verification gap. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 046|ID+C guide p. 46]]
- The O+M practices described here are related operating mechanisms, not a renamed version of the BD+C credit. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 002|O+M p. 2]]
