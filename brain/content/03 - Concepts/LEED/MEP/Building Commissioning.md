# Building Commissioning

## Definition

Building commissioning is a planned verification process that connects owner requirements and design intent with system review, testing, correction, documentation, and handover. LEED v5 applies different commissioning forms to new construction and existing-building operations. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 088|BD+C p. 88]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 060|O+M p. 60]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 061|O+M p. 61]]

## Why It Matters

The BD+C prerequisite is intended to improve energy performance and limit greenhouse-gas emissions by verifying operation against the owner's project requirements, while the O+M credit uses commissioning to improve operations and energy and resource efficiency. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 088|BD+C p. 88]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 060|O+M p. 60]]

The foundational source also presents commissioning and retrocommissioning as a way to arrest performance degradation and restore intended operation over time. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 018|LEED v4 foundation p. 18]]

## Authority and Version Scope

- **BD+C:** Fundamental Commissioning is a current prerequisite, and Enhanced Commissioning is a separate current credit with enhanced MEP, enclosure, and monitoring-based pathways. Captured February 2026 addenda modify commissioning guidance and documentation language. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 088|BD+C p. 88]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 121|BD+C p. 121]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 004|BD+C addenda p. 4]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 006|BD+C addenda p. 6]]
- **ID+C:** The April 2025 launch guide presents fundamental and enhanced commissioning for Commercial Interiors, including interfaces with base-building systems. The current published ID+C rating system is not locally validated, so these provisions remain partially verified. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 127|ID+C guide p. 127]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 212|ID+C guide p. 212]]
- **O+M:** The current rating system offers retro-commissioning or monitoring-based commissioning, and captured addenda clarify the enhanced MBCx point value, RCx system scope, and the minimum data period for the energy information system. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 060|O+M p. 60]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 061|O+M p. 61]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-addenda-table-all-02-20-2026/pages/Page 002|O+M addenda p. 2]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-addenda-table-all-02-20-2026/pages/Page 003|O+M addenda p. 3]]

It is supporting engineering practice here, not a LEED v5 requirement, because no cited current LEED page names it as the compliance standard for these provisions. ## LEED v5 Requirements

### BD+C

EAp3 requires commissioning of systems, controls, and the envelope under the applicable ANSI/ASHRAE/IES Standard 90.1 provisions plus LEED additions. The current page specifies Standard 90.1-2019 or later for registration before 2028-01-01 and 2022 or later on or after that date, designation of a qualified commissioning provider by the end of design development, early assistance with the owner's project requirements, review of the basis of design, and participation in design and construction reviews. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 088|BD+C p. 88]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 089|BD+C p. 89]]

EAc5 provides enhanced pathways, including enhanced MEP commissioning under ANSI/ASHRAE/IES Standard 202-2024, building-enclosure commissioning, and monitoring-based commissioning. The MBCx pathway coordinates the provider, facilities management, trend monitoring, anomaly review, corrective action, and reporting. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 121|BD+C p. 121]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 122|BD+C p. 122]]

The captured BD+C addenda remove one envelope-testing sentence from the fundamental requirement guidance, replace references to an O&M Manual with a Systems Manual in the affected guide content, and revise commissioning-report and representative functional-test documentation. The enhanced-credit addenda also adjust timing guidance and documentation examples without replacing the current rating-system text summarized above. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 004|BD+C addenda p. 4]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 006|BD+C addenda p. 6]]

### ID+C

The launch guide presents EAp3 for project-scope systems and tenant interfaces with base-building systems, using Standard 90.1 commissioning plus LEED provisions for provider qualifications, the owner's project requirements, basis-of-design review, construction review, and contractor-document sampling. It also presents EAc4 enhanced commissioning under Standard 202-2024 for in-scope MEP and related systems. These provisions are not asserted as current published ID+C requirements in this vault. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 127|ID+C guide p. 127]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 128|ID+C guide p. 128]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 212|ID+C guide p. 212]]

The February 2026 ID+C addenda revise launch-guide timing guidance by allowing that, in certain scenarios, later provider engagement may still meet the intent of the Standard 202 timeline. This is guidance-level clarification and does not close the missing-current-rating-system gap. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-id-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 001|ID+C addenda p. 1]]

### O+M

EAc7 offers either retro-commissioning or monitoring-based commissioning. The retro-commissioning path requires a recent process with a qualified third-party provider, an owner representative and operator, owner objectives, a plan, site assessment and functional tests, a list of measures and responsibilities, implementation subject to the stated capital-expense exception, verification, a final report, updated current facilities requirements and O&M plan, and operator training. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 060|O+M p. 60]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 061|O+M p. 61]]

The MBCx path requires a plan for continued MBCx, defined roles, staff training, trend-monitoring technology, correction and verification procedures, annual reporting, and an energy information system; the enhanced path adds provider coordination, faster fault communication, quarterly review, and enhanced software practices. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 061|O+M p. 61]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 062|O+M p. 62]]

Captured addenda correct enhanced MBCx to two points, focus RCx on energy-using systems, and allow the EIS to have at least three months of data when corrective action has begun rather than a complete twelve-month history. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-addenda-table-all-02-20-2026/pages/Page 002|O+M addenda p. 2]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-addenda-table-all-02-20-2026/pages/Page 003|O+M addenda p. 3]]

## MEP and Piping Application

> [!note] Vault-authored engineering interpretation
> The applications below are engineering translations of the cited commissioning scopes. They are not additional LEED deliverables.

- Build the commissioning scope around HVAC, service-water heating, plumbing and process interfaces, power, lighting, controls, renewable-energy interfaces, and envelope interactions that affect system operation. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 088|BD+C p. 88]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 212|ID+C guide p. 212]]
- Convert the owner's project requirements into verifiable sequences, setpoints, alarms, valve and damper positions, sensor behavior, reset logic, and operating modes, then trace observed test results back to those requirements. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 088|BD+C p. 88]]
- Treat hydronic, domestic-water, fuel-gas, drainage, and specialty-piping interfaces as systems with controls, safeties, balancing, access, and maintenance needs rather than as isolated pipe installation. This is an interpretation of the source's whole-system verification model. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-guideline-0-2019/pages/Page 004|Guideline 0 p. 4]]
- Use trend data and fault review to extend commissioning into operations where the selected MBCx path applies. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 061|O+M p. 61]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 062|O+M p. 62]]

## Engineering Workflow

1. **Predesign:** establish the owner's requirements, scope, roles, budget, and initial commissioning plan. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-guideline-0-2019/pages/Page 006|Guideline 0 p. 6]]
2. **Design:** review the basis of design against the owner's requirements and resolve commissioning comments while changes are still practical. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 088|BD+C p. 88]]
3. **Construction:** review relevant submittals and substitutions, coordinate milestone reviews, and prepare representative functional tests for installed systems. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 089|BD+C p. 89]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 004|BD+C addenda p. 4]]
4. **Handover:** close or explicitly plan incomplete items, finalize the commissioning record, update operating information, and confirm operator training. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 004|BD+C addenda p. 4]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 061|O+M p. 61]]
5. **Operation:** use RCx or MBCx to identify faults, implement corrections, verify results, and keep facilities requirements and plans current. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 061|O+M p. 61]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 062|O+M p. 62]]

## Documentation and Evidence

- **BD+C requirement evidence:** owner's project requirements, basis of design review, commissioning reviews, commissioning report, and representative completed functional-test scripts are supported by the cited requirement and addenda; exact submission form should follow the applicable current LEED documentation instructions. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 088|BD+C p. 88]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 004|BD+C addenda p. 4]]
- **O+M requirement evidence:** RCx plans, test and measure records, measure tracking, verification, final report, current facilities requirements, O&M plan, training record, trends, fault logs, and annual MBCx summary are tied to the selected path. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 060|O+M p. 60]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 061|O+M p. 61]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 062|O+M p. 62]]
- **Engineering records, not automatically mandatory:** issue logs, point-to-point checks, sensor calibration records, TAB coordination, valve lineups, screenshots, and deficiency aging can strengthen traceability when they support the selected commissioning scope. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-guideline-0-2019/pages/Page 004|Guideline 0 p. 4]]
