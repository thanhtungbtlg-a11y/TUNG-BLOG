# Energy Metering and Reporting

## Definition

Energy metering and reporting is the measurement, recording, and periodic disclosure of building or tenant energy use and demand so performance can be managed over time. LEED v5 separates the applicable scope and reporting rules by rating system. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 090|BD+C p. 90]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 091|BD+C p. 91]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]]

## Why It Matters

The current BD+C and O+M prerequisites are intended to support energy management and reveal ongoing opportunities for energy and greenhouse-gas savings by tracking energy use and demand. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 090|BD+C p. 90]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]]

Foundational LEED material frames monitoring and verification as the feedback needed to identify design, installation, equipment, and maintenance problems that erode operating performance. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 076|LEED v4 foundation p. 76]]

## LEED v5 Requirements

### BD+C

EAp4 requires New Construction and Core and Shell projects to install or use devices that monitor and record energy under the applicable Standard 90.1 version: 2019 or later before 2028-01-01 and 2022 or later on or after that date. On-site renewable electricity generation must meet the same monitoring and reporting framework used for electrical end uses. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 090|BD+C p. 90]]

Major renovations and buildings eligible for the cited Standard 90.1 exceptions must measure whole-building use for each energy source and peak electrical demand at least monthly. Projects must commit to annual submission of twelve consecutive months of monthly consumption by source, renewable generation, and peak demand, with stated Core and Shell exceptions for future tenant systems. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 090|BD+C p. 90]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 091|BD+C p. 91]]

The captured BD+C addendum clarifies guide documentation for monitoring and recording devices by energy source, including renewable and district energy, and gives plans, schedules, and submittals as examples. It does not replace the rating-system requirement. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 004|BD+C addenda p. 4]]

### ID+C

The April 2025 launch guide applies EAp4 only to specified project scopes, including certain fit-outs, electrical-distribution alterations, new direct energy service, or new on-site renewable energy. It differentiates tenant monitoring and recording by project size and service type and requires annual reporting of twelve months of monthly tenant-metered data. These provisions are guidance-level for this vault until the current published ID+C rating system is validated. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 140|ID+C guide p. 140]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 141|ID+C guide p. 141]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 142|ID+C guide p. 142]]

### O+M

EAp2 requires permanent meters or submeters for total building consumption by energy source; utility meters may provide the aggregate, and unmetered delivered fuels must be tracked by delivery date and amount. Owner-controlled meters must follow the manufacturer's calibration interval, and tenant meters may be excluded for up to ten percent of gross building area when the corresponding area is also excluded from intensity calculations. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]]

The project must report monthly energy use by source for a twelve-month reporting period and commit to ongoing annual reporting of monthly data after certification. The cited O+M prerequisite does not itself require the subsystem and end-use metering described by Standard 90.1 for BD+C. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]]

## MEP and Piping Application

> [!note] Vault-authored engineering interpretation
> The applications below describe a practical metering architecture. They are not additional LEED requirements.

- Define the measurement boundary first: whole building, tenant, energy source, renewable generation, electrical end use, or thermal subsystem. This prevents a source meter from being mistaken for a subsystem diagnostic meter. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 090|BD+C p. 90]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]]
- Coordinate electrical meters with thermal-energy, steam, chilled-water, hot-water, fuel, and renewable-generation measurements so reported totals use consistent boundaries and time periods. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]]
- Integrate appropriate meters with the building management or energy information system for timestamps, trend storage, alarms, and comparison, while keeping those features labeled as engineering practice unless the selected LEED path explicitly requires them. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 076|LEED v4 foundation p. 76]]
- Treat meter range, accuracy, communications, access, commissioning, naming, and calibration responsibility as design inputs because data that cannot be reconciled or maintained is weak operational evidence. This is an engineering interpretation supported by the O+M calibration requirement and Standard 90.1 reporting structure. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-90-1-2022/pages/Page 147|ASHRAE 90.1 p. 147]]

## Engineering Workflow

1. **Set boundaries and obligations:** identify the applicable rating system, project scope, energy sources, tenant exclusions, renewable systems, and required reporting interval. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 090|BD+C p. 90]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]]
2. **Create the meter schedule:** map each required quantity to a device, location, source, units, interval, owner, communications path, and downstream report. This schedule is an engineering interpretation of the device and reporting requirements. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 004|BD+C addenda p. 4]]
3. **Coordinate design:** align one-lines, piping diagrams, controls points, meter specifications, and data architecture with the selected Standard 90.1 and LEED scope. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 090|BD+C p. 90]]
4. **Commission data paths:** verify installation, scaling, timestamps, source labels, aggregation, and representative trends before relying on reports. This is engineering practice that supports the source's monitoring-and-verification purpose. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 076|LEED v4 foundation p. 76]]
5. **Operate and report:** reconcile monthly totals, retain the required reporting record, investigate anomalies, and maintain calibration where the owner controls the meter. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 091|BD+C p. 91]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]]

## Documentation and Evidence

- **BD+C requirement evidence:** device documentation by energy source and the annual reporting data are supported by the rating system and addendum; plans, schedules, and submittals are examples named by the addendum rather than a universal closed list. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 091|BD+C p. 91]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 004|BD+C addenda p. 4]]
- **O+M requirement evidence:** meter inventory, calibration record where applicable, delivered-fuel logs, tenant-exclusion calculation if used, and twelve months of monthly data by source support the cited prerequisite. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]]
- **Engineering records, not automatically mandatory:** metering diagrams, point lists, trend screenshots, data-quality checks, reconciliation worksheets, alarm histories, and exception logs can make the measurement chain auditable. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 076|LEED v4 foundation p. 76]]

## Technical Standards

- **LEED requirement:** current BD+C explicitly requires monitoring and recording under the applicable ANSI/ASHRAE/IES Standard 90.1 version and extends comparable monitoring to on-site renewable electricity. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 090|BD+C p. 90]]
- **Referenced technical method:** Standard 90.1-2022 addresses electrical end-use monitoring and reporting intervals and separately addresses whole-building metering of non-electrical energy sources. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-90-1-2022/pages/Page 147|ASHRAE 90.1 p. 147]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-90-1-2022/pages/Page 177|ASHRAE 90.1 p. 177]]
- **Restriction note:** [[02 - Sources/LEED/Register/ASHRAE Standard 90.1-2022]] is a restricted, annotated local source; this note paraphrases only the two cited metering pages and does not reproduce standard tables or clauses.
