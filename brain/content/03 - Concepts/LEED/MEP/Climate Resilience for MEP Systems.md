# Climate Resilience for MEP Systems

## Definition

Climate resilience for MEP systems is the use of a project-specific hazard assessment to inform how building services resist disruption, protect occupants, maintain critical functions, and recover. LEED v5 establishes formal assessment and response provisions, while the detailed MEP translation remains project-specific engineering work. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 013|BD+C p. 13]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 012|O+M p. 12]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 019|O+M p. 19]]

## Why It Matters

The current BD+C and O+M prerequisites seek to make observed, projected, and future natural hazards visible, reduce vulnerability, and support long-term safety and sustainability. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 013|BD+C p. 13]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 012|O+M p. 12]]

Current credits connect that assessment to resilient site design, occupant thermal safety during power outages, smoke-event operating modes, and emergency plans that protect and restore critical systems. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 051|BD+C p. 51]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 176|BD+C p. 176]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 177|BD+C p. 177]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 019|O+M p. 19]]

## LEED v5 Requirements

### BD+C

IPp1 requires New Construction and Core and Shell projects to assess observed, projected, and future site-specific hazards. The team must identify at least two priority hazards and record the emissions scenario, projected service life, hazard and risk levels, exposure, sensitivity, adaptive capacity, vulnerability, overall risk, and potential impacts; where possible, findings are to inform planning, design, construction, and operation. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 013|BD+C p. 13]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 014|BD+C p. 14]]

SSc4 is an optional two-point credit that applies best practices to at least two highest-priority hazards. Its MEP-relevant provisions include flood protection for critical utilities and piping, emergency or connectable backup power in specified hazard pathways, and water-system measures for drought and flooding. The captured addendum clarifies that selecting Critical Utilities as a flood strategy requires all of its listed sub-bullets. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 051|BD+C p. 51]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 052|BD+C p. 52]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 053|BD+C p. 53]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 003|BD+C addenda p. 3]]

EQc4 is an optional Resilient Spaces credit. Its current pathways include an episodic outdoor-event management mode for conditions such as wildland-fire smoke, thermal modeling for habitable safety zones during a two-day peak summer or winter power outage, and operable-window provisions under the cited conditions. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 176|BD+C p. 176]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 177|BD+C p. 177]]

### ID+C

The April 2025 launch guide presents IPp1 as a required Commercial Interiors assessment of observed, projected, and future hazards, with at least two priorities and the same core risk factors used by BD+C. Its guidance emphasizes interior systems, occupant safety, operational continuity, and interfaces with the larger building. These provisions are not asserted as current published ID+C requirements in this vault. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 031|ID+C guide p. 31]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 032|ID+C guide p. 32]]

### O+M

IPp1 requires an existing building to assess current and future hazards, select at least two priority hazards for operations and maintenance strategies, and record the specified scenario, service-life, hazard, exposure, vulnerability, and risk information. Where possible, the assessment is to inform operations and maintenance. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 012|O+M p. 12]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 013|O+M p. 13]]

IPc1 is an optional one-point credit requiring an emergency response plan tied to the priority hazards. The plan covers responsible personnel, training and drills, communications, traffic control, vulnerable populations, protection and restoration of critical facilities and systems, backup power for command centers and essential systems, maintenance, and communication to relevant parties. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 019|O+M p. 19]]

EQc6 is an optional one-point credit for either an episodic outdoor-event management mode, including wildland-fire smoke, or an infection-risk management mode; the selected readiness plan is incorporated into the current facilities requirements and O+M plan. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 082|O+M p. 82]]

## MEP and Piping Application

> [!note] Vault-authored engineering interpretation
> The applications below translate identified hazards into design and operating questions. Except where a cited LEED pathway states otherwise, they are not LEED thresholds or mandatory strategies.

- **Power loss:** classify essential loads, required operating duration, transfer arrangements, fuel or energy constraints, and restart sequences; coordinate critical HVAC, water, controls, communications, and life-safety interfaces. Current LEED sources explicitly address backup power in O+M emergency planning and in selected BD+C resilience pathways. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 019|O+M p. 19]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 053|BD+C p. 53]]
- **Extreme heat or cold:** test cooling, heating, ventilation, controls, and designated safety zones against the selected hazard scenario; passive survivability is a formal BD+C option only under the cited thermal-safety pathway, not a universal prerequisite. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 013|BD+C p. 13]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 177|BD+C p. 177]]
- **Flooding:** review equipment elevations, penetrations, floodproofed enclosures, anchorage, backflow and infiltration risks, sanitary and storm drainage, storage tanks, and recovery access. Some of these are explicit SSc4 strategies when that credit and hazard path are selected. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 052|BD+C p. 52]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 053|BD+C p. 53]]
- **Water interruption or drought:** evaluate potable, process, cooling, and sanitation dependencies, then consider isolation, storage, demand reduction, or alternative supply appropriate to the assessed risk. This is engineering interpretation derived from drought assessment and water-efficiency pathways, not a stated universal LEED resilience requirement. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 013|BD+C p. 13]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 051|BD+C p. 51]]
- **Wildfire smoke or other episodic outdoor conditions:** define filtration, outdoor-air, pressure, sensor, damper, control-mode, communication, and commissioning needs appropriate to the event. Current BD+C and O+M credits explicitly address an episodic outdoor-event management mode, but the detailed system design must follow the applicable project and standard. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 176|BD+C p. 176]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 082|O+M p. 82]]
- **Redundancy and recovery:** identify single points of failure, manual overrides, spare capacity, safe shutdown, restart order, temporary connections, and access for repair. These are vault-authored methods for satisfying project-specific resilience objectives, not generic LEED mandates. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 014|BD+C p. 14]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 019|O+M p. 19]]

## Engineering Workflow

1. **Assess:** define the service life and scenario, identify relevant hazards, and rank at least two priorities under the applicable assessment. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 013|BD+C p. 13]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 012|O+M p. 12]]
2. **Map dependencies:** connect each priority hazard to utilities, plant, distribution, controls, occupied zones, critical loads, operators, and external services. This is an engineering interpretation of the required impact and vulnerability analysis. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 013|BD+C p. 13]]
3. **Select strategies:** compare passive, active, redundant, operational, and recovery measures, then distinguish prerequisites from optional-credit paths and owner-selected resilience goals. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 051|BD+C p. 51]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 176|BD+C p. 176]]
4. **Document and commission:** incorporate selected modes and protections into drawings, sequences, commissioning scope, facilities requirements, and emergency procedures where the cited credit requires them. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 176|BD+C p. 176]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 019|O+M p. 19]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 082|O+M p. 82]]
5. **Exercise and update:** train responsible staff, test response modes, record deficiencies, and revise the plan as conditions, systems, and responsibilities change. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 019|O+M p. 19]]

## Documentation and Evidence

- **Assessment evidence:** hazard sources, emissions scenario, projected service life, risk and vulnerability ratings, priority-hazard selection, impact narrative, and considered strategies are supported by the current prerequisites. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 013|BD+C p. 13]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 014|BD+C p. 14]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 012|O+M p. 12]]
- **Selected-credit evidence:** drawings and specifications for critical utilities, backup-power and transfer provisions, thermal models and safety-zone designation, event-mode sequences and commissioning records, or an emergency response plan should be retained when the corresponding cited pathway is pursued. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 052|BD+C p. 52]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 053|BD+C p. 53]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 176|BD+C p. 176]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 177|BD+C p. 177]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 019|O+M p. 19]]
- **Engineering records, not automatically mandatory:** dependency diagrams, failure-mode registers, outage simulations, restart matrices, temporary-connection plans, spare-parts strategies, and recovery checklists can make MEP resilience decisions auditable. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 019|O+M p. 19]]

## Technical Standards

- **LEED-referenced method:** current BD+C and O+M Resilient Spaces pages reference ASHRAE Guideline 44 for episodic outdoor-event management. A validated local Guideline 44 source is not available, so this note records the relationship but does not restate the standard's method. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 176|BD+C p. 176]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 082|O+M p. 82]]
- **LEED-referenced method:** the current BD+C operable-window pathway cites ASHRAE 62.1-2022 Section 6.4. The relationship is confirmed, but the pathway applies only when that option is pursued. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 177|BD+C p. 177]]
- **No inferred mandate:** these references do not make either technical standard a universal climate-resilience requirement outside the cited pathway.
