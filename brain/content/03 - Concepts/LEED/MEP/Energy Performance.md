# Energy Performance

## Definition

Energy performance is the relationship between a building's energy demand, the efficiency of its systems and controls, and measured operating results. LEED v5 addresses this differently in design systems and in O+M: BD+C establishes minimum and enhanced design-performance paths, while O+M evaluates measured existing-building performance. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 085|BD+C p. 85]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 104|BD+C p. 104]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 039|O+M p. 39]]

## Why It Matters

The cited LEED v5 credits connect lower energy use with reduced environmental damage, air pollution, greenhouse-gas emissions, and economic harm. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 085|BD+C p. 85]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 048|O+M p. 48]]

Foundational LEED guidance treats efficient systems, controls, energy modeling, and ongoing monitoring as complementary parts of maintaining intended performance. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 075|LEED v4 foundation p. 75]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 076|LEED v4 foundation p. 76]]

## LEED v5 Requirements

### BD+C

The Minimum Energy Efficiency prerequisite uses edition- and registration-date-specific Standard 90.1 compliance paths. This note does not reproduce the associated factors, tables, or thresholds. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 085|BD+C p. 85]]

Enhanced Energy Efficiency offers prescriptive and simulation pathways. The prescriptive material identifies HVAC and service-water-heating measures among eligible regulated-load improvements; the simulation pathway evaluates whole-building performance. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 104|BD+C p. 104]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 105|BD+C p. 105]]

The captured addendum changes reference-guide guidance about exceptions to mandatory provisions; it does not replace the published rating-system requirement. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-addenda-table-only-02-20-2026/pages/Page 003|BD+C addenda p. 3]]

### ID+C

**Source-gated:** the April 2025 launch guide describes minimum-efficiency paths for Commercial Interiors and applies Standard 90.1 to the project scope, but these requirements are retained as guidance-level until a current published ID+C rating system is validated. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 113|ID+C guide p. 113]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 114|ID+C guide p. 114]]

The same guide presents enhanced-efficiency options for interior project scope. No numeric threshold from that guide is promoted here as a current published requirement. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773520-leed-v5-id-c-reference-guide-launch-edition-1/pages/Page 172|ID+C guide p. 172]]

### O+M

The Minimum Energy Performance prerequisite selects among measured-performance paths based on property eligibility and available comparison methods. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 039|O+M p. 39]]

Optimized Energy Performance builds on the same measured paths and awards performance beyond the prerequisite. This note intentionally omits scores, baselines, and point thresholds. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 048|O+M p. 48]]

## MEP and Piping Application

> [!note] Vault-authored engineering interpretation
> The applications below translate the cited source scope into coordination practices; they are not additional LEED requirements.

- Reduce loads before selecting equipment, then coordinate HVAC efficiency, service-water heating, pumps, and control sequences as one performance model. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 104|BD+C p. 104]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 105|BD+C p. 105]]
- Preserve the assumptions that connect equipment schedules, control narratives, energy models, and commissioning tests so the design pathway remains auditable. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 075|LEED v4 foundation p. 75]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 076|LEED v4 foundation p. 76]]
- Use meters and trends to compare operating results with intended performance, while treating detailed metering obligations as the separate concept [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Energy Metering and Reporting]]. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 076|LEED v4 foundation p. 76]]

## Engineering Workflow

1. Confirm the rating system, project scope, registration timing, and selected compliance path. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 085|BD+C p. 85]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 039|O+M p. 39]]
2. Establish loads and model boundaries before comparing HVAC, service-water-heating, pump, and control alternatives. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 104|BD+C p. 104]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 105|BD+C p. 105]]
3. Carry selected measures into schedules, sequences, submittals, and commissioning evidence. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 075|LEED v4 foundation p. 75]]
4. For existing buildings, compile the measured record required by the selected O+M pathway and investigate performance drift. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 039|O+M p. 39]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 048|O+M p. 48]]

## Documentation and Evidence

- **Published-requirement evidence:** selected compliance path, model or prescriptive documentation, equipment and controls records, and the project-specific evidence required by the applicable rating-system pages. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 085|BD+C p. 85]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 104|BD+C p. 104]]
- **Operational evidence:** the measured performance record used by the selected O+M path. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 039|O+M p. 39]]
- **Engineering evidence:** model-input register, equipment schedules, control narratives, trend reviews, and issue logs can connect design assumptions to operating outcomes. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 076|LEED v4 foundation p. 76]]
