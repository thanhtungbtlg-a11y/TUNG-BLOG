# LEED v4 to v5 High-Level Crosswalk

> [!warning] Scope boundary
> This is a category and major-theme map, not a credit-by-credit crosswalk. `Changed` means that the validated local sources visibly frame or organize the theme differently; it does not establish a detailed requirement change.

> [!info] Official comparison evidence
> [[02 - Sources/LEED/Register/LEED BD+C v4 to v5 Summary of Changes]] is now the primary source for the high-level BD+C comparison. Its detailed category tables are preserved as page images, so they support the themes below but are not being transcribed into a credit-by-credit crosswalk.
