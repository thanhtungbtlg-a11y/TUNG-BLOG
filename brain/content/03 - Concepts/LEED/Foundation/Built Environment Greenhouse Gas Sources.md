# Built Environment Greenhouse Gas Sources

## Definition

Built-environment greenhouse-gas sources include building systems and energy use, transportation, water use and treatment, land-cover change, materials, and construction. The guide treats buildings and land use as connected parts of the emissions problem. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 015|PDF page 15]]

## Why it matters

Focusing only on design and construction can omit emissions associated with location and transportation. The guide's office-building example shows that transport can form a large share of project-associated emissions. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 015|PDF page 15]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 016|PDF page 16]]

## How it works

Carbon emissions can serve as a common metric across energy, water, solid waste, materials, and transportation. The source also warns that carbon is not a complete sustainability goal because indoor air quality, human health, and habitat protection still require separate attention. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 017|PDF page 17]]

## LEED relevance

The guide connects LEED and green-building strategies with emissions reduction while retaining broader environmental and human goals. This introductory section does not define a current carbon threshold or credit. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 015|PDF page 15]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 017|PDF page 17]]

## Application to MEP and piping engineering

> [!note] Practical engineering interpretation
> This section is an engineering interpretation, not a LEED requirement stated by the source.

- Build an emissions boundary that states which energy, water, refrigerant, material, construction, and transport effects are included.
- Use carbon as one comparison metric while retaining separate health, habitat, water, and service-quality checks.
