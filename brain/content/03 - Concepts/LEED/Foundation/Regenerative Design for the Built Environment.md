# Regenerative Design for the Built Environment

## Definition

Regenerative design seeks built environments that participate as a positive developmental force in local ecosystems and communities rather than only reducing damage. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 022|PDF page 22]]

## Why it matters

The guide argues that doing less environmental and health damage is not the endpoint of sustainability. It describes regenerative projects as supporting community and ecosystem health and contributing to resource renewal. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 022|PDF page 22]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 023|PDF page 23]]

## How it works

The source emphasizes understanding a place's patterns and living systems, involving stakeholders, supporting diversity, and creating beneficial resource relationships. Its examples include exporting excess electricity, returning cleaner water, producing food, supporting community networks, and regenerating biodiversity. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 022|PDF page 22]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 023|PDF page 23]]

## LEED relevance

The guide places regenerative design beyond damage reduction and beyond routine checklist use. These pages describe an aspirational direction, not a current LEED requirement. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 022|PDF page 22]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 023|PDF page 23]]

## Application to MEP and piping engineering

> [!note] Practical engineering interpretation
> This section is an engineering interpretation, not a LEED requirement stated by the source.

- Ask whether energy and water systems can create a verified benefit for connected site or community systems, not merely reduce consumption.
- Define the receiving system and measurement boundary before describing an outcome as regenerative.
