## Duplicate-Control Rules

- Do not create a v5 note by renaming or overwriting a LEED v4 foundational note.
- Compare the candidate with every listed possible duplicate before drafting.
- Prefer one atomic note per engineering idea; keep rating-system-specific requirements in version-labeled sections or separate notes when they diverge.
- Mark `current_requirements_verified: true` only after the comparison is documented.
