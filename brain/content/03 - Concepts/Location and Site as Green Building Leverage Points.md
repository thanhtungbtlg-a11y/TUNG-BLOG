# Location and Site as Green Building Leverage Points

## Definition

Location and site decisions are early leverage points because they shape transportation, infrastructure, habitat, water, heat, public health, and community outcomes before building systems are selected.

## Sourced Facts

- The guide states that where a building is located can be as important as how it is built, particularly through travel choices, community connection, and greenhouse-gas effects. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 059|PDF p. 59; guide p. 52]]
- It recommends evaluating prior development, infrastructure and transit connections, street life, and how people travel between home and work. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 059|PDF p. 59; guide p. 52]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 060|PDF p. 60; guide p. 53]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 061|PDF p. 61; guide p. 54]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 062|PDF p. 62; guide p. 55]]
- Site design is framed through relationships to the bioregion, watershed, habitat, climate, and community, with rainwater management and habitat conservation as examples of wider benefits. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 063|PDF p. 63; guide p. 56]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 064|PDF p. 64; guide p. 57]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 065|PDF p. 65; guide p. 58]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 066|PDF p. 66; guide p. 59]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 067|PDF p. 67; guide p. 60]]

## Interpretation

> [!note] Interpretation
> Establish location and site constraints before optimizing building equipment. Some loads, water demands, infrastructure needs, and transport impacts can be avoided through planning choices and cannot be fully corrected by efficient MEP systems later.
