# Continuous Performance Feedback in Building Operations

## Definition

Continuous performance feedback is the operating practice of comparing measured conditions with explicit goals, finding causes of deviation, and applying corrective action throughout a building's life.

## Sourced Facts

- The guide says useful performance data should provide honest feedback on sustainability goals rather than merely serve as favorable proof. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 054|PDF p. 54; guide p. 47]]
- It recommends recurring education, inspections, maintenance, retrocommissioning, energy and water audits, waste audits, and occupant surveys. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 054|PDF p. 54; guide p. 47]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 055|PDF p. 55; guide p. 48]]
- For energy performance, it connects owner requirements, commissioning, monitoring, benchmarking, staff training, preventive maintenance, and occupant engagement. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 076|PDF p. 76; guide p. 69]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-coreconcepts-strategies-3rd-edition/pages/Page 077|PDF p. 77; guide p. 70]]

## Interpretation

> [!note] Interpretation
> Treat operations as part of design completion, not as a handoff after design. A useful feedback loop has a target, a measured variable, a responsible owner, a review cadence, and a defined corrective response.
