# LEED Addenda

## Registered Role

Use the [official LEED Addenda Database](https://www.usgbc.org/resources/leed-addenda-database) to track corrections and interpretations that may change how a registered rating system or guide is applied. Three dated local tables are now validated:

- [[02 - Sources/LEED/Register/LEED v5 BD+C Addenda - 2026-02-20]]
- [[02 - Sources/LEED/Register/LEED v5 ID+C Addenda - 2026-02-20]]
- [[02 - Sources/LEED/Register/LEED v5 O+M Addenda - 2026-02-20]]

## Capture Rule

Record each relevant addendum with its identifier, publication date, LEED version, rating system, affected source or credit, and official URL. A source cannot be treated as current solely because its original PDF was validated; applicable addenda must also be checked.

The dated tables reviewed on 2026-07-22 contain corrections and amendments but no rows separately labeled as LEED Interpretations. Preserve that distinction unless an official interpretation identifier is present.
