# ASHRAE Guideline 0-2019

## Identity And Scope

The title page confirms ASHRAE Guideline 0-2019, superseding the 2013 edition: [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-guideline-0-2019/pages/Page 001|PDF page 1]]. Its purpose and scope describe a commissioning process from predesign through occupancy and operation: [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-guideline-0-2019/pages/Page 004|PDF page 4]].

## LEED Boundary

Commissioning is relevant across LEED systems, but the validated LEED v5 corpus reviewed here references ASHRAE Standard 202 rather than Guideline 0. Do not claim Guideline 0-2019 is required by LEED v5 without an explicit LEED page.
