## Authority And Version Rules

1. Preserve every validated source and its original version scope.
2. Read published rating-system requirement text before implementation guidance.
3. Apply dated addenda only after checking whether the target rating system or reference-guide edition already incorporates each row.
4. Treat reference-guide guidance as edition-specific and subordinate to a later conflicting published rating system until the relationship is reviewed.
5. Treat referenced technical standards as authoritative within their own scope, but do not claim LEED applicability without an explicit LEED source page.
6. Treat foundational sources as durable provenance, not automatically current requirement text.
7. Treat scorecards, summaries, and impact views as navigation aids, not complete requirement text.
8. Never use draft or public-comment material as final project-compliance authority.
9. Label vault-authored MEP interpretation as interpretation and keep its supporting source trail visible.
10. Do not mark a concept obsolete without a newer authoritative source that supports that conclusion.
11. Keep paid or restricted content private, quote minimally, and never redistribute original source content.

See [[03 - Concepts/LEED/LEED Version and Authority Map]] for the operational hierarchy.
