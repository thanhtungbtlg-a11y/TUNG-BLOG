##### **REQUIREMENTS EXPLAINED**


This credit rewards decreased regulated and unregulated energy usage below the _ASHRAE_
_90.1-2019_ requirements referenced in _EAp2: Minimum Energy Efficiency_, Option 1. To limit
documentation level of effort, use the same _ASHRAE_ 90.1 compliance method for prerequisite
and credit compliance — either the prescriptive method or Appendix G Performance Rating
Method (PRM).

For the prescriptive method, use the _ASHRAE 90.1-2022_ standard for maximum achievement of
points, and to earn 4 New Construction points or 2 Core and Shell points automatically per
_EAc3: Enhanced Energy Efficiency_, Option 1 Path 1 Case 2.
