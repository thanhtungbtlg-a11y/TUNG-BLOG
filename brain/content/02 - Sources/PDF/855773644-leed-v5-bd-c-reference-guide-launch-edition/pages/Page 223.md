**ASHRAE 90.1 Mandatory Provisions**


All projects must meet applicable mandatory provisions from the referenced version of _ASHRAE_
_90.1_, found in Sections 5.4 (Building Envelope), 6.4 (HVAC), 7.4 (Service Water Heating), 8.4
(Power), 9.4 (Lighting), and 10.4 (Other Equipment).
