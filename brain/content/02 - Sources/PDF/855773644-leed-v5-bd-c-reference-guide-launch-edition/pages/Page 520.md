Table 1. Accessibility for physical diversity strategies











|Accessibility for physical diversity|Examples, specifications, and explanation|
|---|---|
|Wave-to-open or vertical hand/foot<br>press door operators at all regularly<br>used building entrances.|No-touch door activation devices accommodate a wide<br>range of users, including those with mobility impairments,<br>people carrying objects or children, and individuals with<br>temporary injuries.|
|Meeting spaces to accommodate<br>mobility devices for at least 10% of<br>occupants.|Accommodations include, but are not limited to, clear floor<br>spaces, and pathways of travel without obstruction. Users<br>must be able to park mobility devices next to or within the<br>seating area to avoid exclusion.|
|Accessible and inclusive equipment<br>and activities in fitness facilities.<br>And<br>Open and accessible route to and<br>around the equipment.|Projects with fitness equipment and activities must be<br>designed to accommodate a range of physical abilities. This<br>includes providing an inclusive range of strength and<br>stretching training equipment.<br> <br>A minimum clearance of 36 inches (0.915 meters) is the<br>appropriate threshold for accessible routes between<br>exercise equipment, as this aligns with ADA requirements<br>for accessible paths and allows adequate space for<br>wheelchair users to navigate between equipment.|
|Alternate accessible route that starts<br>and terminates at the same location<br>for non-accessible routes.|Directness and proximity of the accessible route are<br>essential to maintain a similar level of convenience to the<br>non-accessible route. The alternative accessible route must<br>be in the same general area as the circulation paths.<br> <br>As per_International Code Council_ (ICC), the maximum<br>allowable difference between the start and end points of an<br>accessible route and its non-accessible counterpart is 200<br>feet (61 meters) in most indoor facilities, though it’s<br>recommended to minimize this distance for those with<br>mobility challenges, ideally to 100 feet (30 meters).185|
