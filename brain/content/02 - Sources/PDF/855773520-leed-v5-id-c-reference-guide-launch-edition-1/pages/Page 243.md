## Extracted Content

U.S. projects that document GreenChill certification earn points based on the level of
certification achieved. International projects must document compliance with each requirement
outlined on the EPA GreenChill website for the targeted certification level.

##### **DOCUMENTATION**






|Project<br>types|Options|Paths|Documentation|
|---|---|---|---|
|<br>Interior<br>Design<br>and<br>Construction|All<br>|All<br>|Mechanical schedules (or similar) that show equipment<br>type, capacity, quantity, refrigerant type, and maximum<br>refrigerant charge, or evidence that the project meets<br>cooling, heating, and other project loads without<br>refrigerants.<br>|
|<br>Interior<br>Design<br>and<br>Construction|Option 1<br>|Path 2<br>|<br>Weighted average GWP Benchmark calculation<br>(Equation 3).<br>|
|<br>Interior<br>Design<br>and<br>Construction|Option 1<br>|Path 2<br>|<br>Weighted average GWP calculation from_EAp5:_<br>_Fundamental Refrigerant Management_ <br>|
|<br>Interior<br>Design<br>and<br>Construction|Option 2<br>|All<br>|<br>Confirmation of project scope (initial build-out or<br>renovation, and, if initial build-out, percent of capacity of<br>refrigerant-using equipment installed in the project<br>scope of work).<br>|
|<br>Interior<br>Design<br>and<br>Construction|Option 2<br>|All<br>|<br>Documentation of compliance with field-installed piping<br>limits.<br>|
|<br>Interior<br>Design<br>and<br>Construction|Option 2<br>|All<br>|<br>Contractor documentation demonstrating that<br>installation complies with credit criteria.<br>|
|<br>Interior<br>Design<br>and<br>Construction|Option 2<br>|All<br>|<br>Refrigerant Maintenance Plan that describes how the<br>refrigerant-using systems will be maintained. The plan<br>must identify the designated responsible party for<br>implementing the plan.<br>|
|<br>Interior<br>Design<br>and<br>Construction|Option 2<br>|All<br>|<br>Plans, specifications, or field photos confirming<br>automatic leak detection, if applicable.<br>|
|<br>Interior<br>Design<br>and<br>Construction|Option 3|All|<br>Demonstrate that the project has achieved EPA’s<br>GreenChill Certification.<br>|
|<br>Interior<br>Design<br>and<br>Construction|Option 3|All|<br>Compliance documentation demonstrating that the<br>project meets relevant GreenChill requirements for the<br>certification level specified.|
