##### **REQUIREMENTS EXPLAINED**

This credit rewards increased energy efficiency addressing regulated and unregulated systems
in the project scope, and improved base building performance _._ To limit documentation level of
effort, use the same _ASHRAE_ _90.1_ compliance method for prerequisite and credit compliance:
either the prescriptive method or Appendix G PRM.
