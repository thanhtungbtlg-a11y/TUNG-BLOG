### **MATERIALS AND RESOURCES**

Materials and Resources Credit
### **WASTE REDUCTION PERFORMANCE**


**MRc1**

1–12 points

### **INTENT**

To prevent waste and reduce the amount of materials from building operations and
maintenance disposed of in landfills or incinerators.

### **REQUIREMENTS**

Divert materials generated from building operations and maintenance activities from reaching
landfills, incineration (waste-to-energy), or other disposal methods. Diversion includes strategies
such as waste prevention (reduction), reuse, recycling, or composting. Points are awarded
