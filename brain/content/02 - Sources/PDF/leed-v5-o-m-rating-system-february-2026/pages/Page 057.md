## Extracted Content

|Phase|Action|
|---|---|
|Planning<br>|**Conduct a design charette**: This may be in-person, virtual, or hybrid, and should<br>include as many team members as possible. Present materials developed in the pre-<br>planning stage. Discuss conceptual plans, issues that make them unfeasible, how to<br>overcome the obstacles, possible changes to the plans, and other ideas to consider.<br>|
|Planning<br>|<br>**Develop multiple decarbonization options**: Building on the charette discussion,<br>develop at least two decarbonization options, one of which must reduce on-site<br>combustion emissions by >90%. Each option must include a narrative and a list of<br>measures, with the timeline and projected impact of each measure on energy and<br>carbon emissions and realistic costs of the decarbonization measures, including<br>architectural, structural, and code costs incurred.<br>|
|Planning<br>|<br>**Create the SDP**: Develop the SDP the project intends to pursue, which may be a<br>decarbonization option from above, an amendment of either, or a new plan. The plan<br>must include a narrative and a list of measures, with the timeline and projected impact<br>of each measure on energy and carbon emissions, and realistic costs of the<br>decarbonization measures, including architectural, structural, and code costs incurred.<br>|
|Planning<br>|<br>**Create a five-year capital plan**: Include SDP measures to be pursued in the next five<br>years. Develop the five-year plan sufficiently for accurate carbon assessments and<br>budgeting. The five-year plan must include a list of measures, with the timeline, cost,<br>and projected impact of each measure on energy and carbon emissions.<br>|
|Attestation and<br>commitment<br>|<br>**Attestation**: The owner or owner’s representative must attest that they have reviewed<br>the SDP and the five-year capital plan. <br>|
|Attestation and<br>commitment<br>|<br>**Commitment**: Provide documentation of the owner’s commitment to implement the<br>measures in the five-year plan, with changes limited to minor modifications, more<br>ambitious implementation, or delays outside of the owner’s control.|


**Visualization of plans**
From the information provided, USGBC will generate graphs of the carbon and energy
trajectories of the SDPs.

**Option 2. Carbon-Neutral and Energy-Efficient (4 points)**

Demonstrate carbon neutrality and high performance by achieving 12 points in _EAc1:_
_Greenhouse Gas Emissions Reduction Performance_ and 12 points in _EAc2: Optimize Energy_
_Performance_ .
