## Extracted Content

- **Refrigerant leakage tracking:** For the 12-month reporting period, track and report, by
refrigerant type and weight, the project’s refrigerant recharge or total procurement of
refrigerants, and report the total GWP of the leaked refrigerants. For initial certifications,
implement refrigerant tracking for no less than the last three months of the 12-month
reporting period and report all recharge occurring during refrigerant leakage inspection
and recharge.


U.S. Green Building Council
LEED v5 Reference Guide for Operations and Maintenance, November 2025 Edition 36
