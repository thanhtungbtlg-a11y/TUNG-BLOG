### **OCCUPANT EXPERIENCE PERFORMANCE**

**EQc3**

1–3 points

### **INTENT**

To evaluate the building’s performance for occupants in terms of comfort, customization, joy,
and belonging and to better understand ways to achieve consistent satisfaction.

### **REQUIREMENTS**

|Achievement Pathways|Points|
|---|---|
|<br>Existing Buildings <br>|<br>1–3<br>|
|<br>Occupant Experience Survey<br>|<br>1–3|



Conduct an occupant experience survey of the building occupants to assess their experience
and satisfaction with the indoor environment during the 12-month reporting period.

Points are awarded according to Table 1.

Table 1. Points for Occupant Experience Survey

|Survey Action|Points|
|---|---|
|<br>> 60% of occupants are satisfied<br>|<br>1 <br>|
|<br>> 80% of occupants are satisfied<br>|<br>2 <br>|
|<br>> 90% of occupants are satisfied<br>|<br>3|



**Required Survey Methodology**

  - Regular building occupants must be surveyed. Surveying visitors is optional.

  - Use the following survey question (or similar): “Indicate how satisfied you are with the
indoor environment in this building.” Additional questions are optional but encouraged.

  - Use a seven-point response scale (e.g., very dissatisfied, dissatisfied, somewhat
dissatisfied, neither satisfied nor dissatisfied, somewhat satisfied, satisfied, and very
satisfied).

  - Calculate the percentage of occupants that are satisfied, mean satisfaction level, and
response variance.

`o` When calculating the percentage of occupants that are satisfied, only include

occupants that respond somewhat satisfied (5), satisfied (6), and very satisfied (7).

  - Meet survey response rates in Appendix III.
