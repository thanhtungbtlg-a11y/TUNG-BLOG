**Option 1. Facility Maintenance Performance (1–2 points)**

Conduct an audit to determine the maintenance level of the facility during the 12-month
reporting period. Points are awarded according to Table 1.

LEVEL 3: MANAGED MAINTENANCE

Level 3 is representative of an average building. Building equipment and components are
functional but occasionally break down. Response times to service and maintenance calls are
inconsistent. Equipment is upgraded on an as-needed basis. Overall the facility shows a basic
level of care. At least 50% of maintenance activities are considered proactive (conducted before
failure and malfunction).

LEVEL 2: COMPREHENSIVE MAINTENANCE

Level 2 is representative of an above average building. Building equipment and components are
functional and in operating condition. Response times to service and maintenance calls are
consistent and timely. Equipment is upgraded regularly and meets current standards. Overall
the facility shows an enhanced level of care. At least 75% of maintenance activities are
considered proactive (conducted before failure and malfunction).
