<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Construction and Demolition Waste Management</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Credit renamed to: MRc Construction and Demolition Waste Diversion.</li>
<li>Added requirement for developing Construction and Demolition Materials Management Plan.</li>
<li>Added list of diversion strategies and rules for calculating diversion rates:
<ul>
<li>Materials source-separated and sent for single-material recycling or industry/manufacturer take-back programs are considered 100% diverted.</li>
<li>Salvaged materials are valued at a 200% diversion rate.</li>
<li>Mixed C&amp;D materials sent to processing facility must assume 35% diversion rate unless the facility has USGBC approved third party verification of recycling rates.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Revised point thresholds for C&amp;D diversion.</li>
<li>Added requirement for a percentage of diverted materials to be salvaged or source-separated and sent to single-material recycler(s) to achieve points.</li>
<li>Eliminated requirements for number of waste streams to be diverted.</li>
<li>Eliminated Option 2, Reduction of Total Waste Material.</li>
<li>Added requirement for CS projects to include approved C&amp;D waste management plan in tenant guidelines.</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
