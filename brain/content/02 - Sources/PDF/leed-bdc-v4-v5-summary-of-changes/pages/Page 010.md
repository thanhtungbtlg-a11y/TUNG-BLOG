<td><strong>Demand Response</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Credit renamed to: EAc Grid Interactive.</li>
<li>Can use a combination of options 1 through 4.</li>
<li>Option 1: Energy Storage (Added).</li>
<li>Option 2: Demand Response Program.</li>
<li>Option 3: Automated Demand Side Management via System or BAS.</li>
<li>Option 4: Power Resilience for critical systems (Added).</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Renewable Energy Production</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Credit renamed to: EAc Renewable Energy.</li>
<li>New calculation method based on Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 renewables.</li>
<li>Projects may combine Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 renewable options to achieve points.</li>
<li>Still subject to contract length, vintage, location, and EAC/REC ownership requirements.</li>
<li>Core + Shell projects have the option to incorporate renewable ready design.</li>
</ul>
</td>
