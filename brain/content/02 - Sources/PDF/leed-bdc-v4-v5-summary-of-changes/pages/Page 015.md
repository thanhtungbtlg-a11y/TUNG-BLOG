<td><strong>Thermal Comfort</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Incorporated into new credit: EQc Occupant Experience.</li>
<li>Updated reference standard to ASHRAE 55-2023.</li>
<li>Modified thermal controllability to a new option: Adaptable Environment.</li>
<li>Added a requirement to explain how the design considers thermal conditions that adjust with seasons, overcooling during nontemperate seasons, and support for occupants carrying out different tasks with varying levels of movement.</li>
<li>Removed ISO and CEN standards option.</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Interior Lighting</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Incorporated into new credit: EQc Occupant Experience.</li>
<li>Condensed and modified requirements for the lighting quality option including a luminance of less than 6,000 candela per square meter, achieve a unified glare rating (UGR) of 19 or lower, and have a CRI of 90 or higher.</li>
<li>Modified lighting control option to a new option: Adaptable Environment.</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Daylight</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Incorporated into new credit: EQc Occupant Experience.</li>
<li>Moved glare-control devices in all regularly occupied spaces in a new pathway.</li>
<li>Added proximity to windows for daylight access in a new pathway.</li>
<li>Modified point and sDA300/50% or sDA150/50% thresholds for daylight simulation (Spatial Daylight Autonomy and Annual Sunlight Exposure) option and added a requirement for any regularly occupied spaces with ASEnet(1000,250) greater than 20% to identify how the space is addressing glare.</li>
<li>Removed simulation: illuminance calculations and measurement options.</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Quality Views</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Incorporated into new credit: EQc Occupant Experience Option 1 path 2.</li>
<li>Reduced the requirements and types of views and increased the point thresholds (75% of regularly occupied floor area with compliant views for 2 points, and 90% for 3 points).</li>
</ul>
</td>
