<td><strong>Outdoor Water Use</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Incorporated into new credit: WEc Enhanced Water Efficiency.</li>
<li>Calculated baseline changed from “peak watering month” to “annual theoretical irrigation requirement (TIR).”</li>
<li>CS projects can earn up to 3 points for Outdoor Water Use reduction with a 75% reduction threshold added.</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Indoor Water Use</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Incorporated into new credit: WEc Enhanced Water Efficiency.</li>
<li>Point thresholds for calculated water reduction revised (1-3 points) for both NC and CS projects.</li>
<li>Added new Option 1. Whole Project Water Use and Option 6. Water Reuse.</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Cooling Tower Water Use</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Incorporated into new credit: WEc Enhanced Water Efficiency, Option 5: Optimize Process Water Use.</li>
<li>Maximum concentrations for parameters in condenser water lowered for NC and CS.</li>
<li>Requirements for earning points for cooling tower cycles revised:
<ul>
<li>1 point: Removed “up to maximum of 10 cycles”.</li>
<li>2 points: Refers to “maximum calculated number of cycles to earn 1 point” without specifying a number. New requirement for increasing number of cycles by at least 25%. Includes both treatment and maintenance in condenser or makeup water systems. Uses the term “alternative water” instead of “recycled nonpotable water.”</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Water Metering</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Credit renamed to Water Metering and Leak Detection.</li>
<li>Add new option (1 point) for leak detection sensors.</li>
<li>Boilers and reclaimed water subsystems removed.</li>
<li>Makeup water subsystem added to cover domestic hot water, swimming pools, chilled water systems, and process water systems.</li>
<li>Commercial kitchen and laundry subsystems added.</li>
<li>Projects are now required to install meters for each applicable subsystem.</li>
<li>Irrigation: 80% requirement removed.</li>
<li>Indoor Plumbing: Specifics added about including/excluding certain fixture.</li>
<li>Added requirement specific to residential projects to meter each dwelling unit .</li>
<li>Requirement added for providing real-time access to data for facility managers/tenants and hourly data recording capability.</li>
<li>Requirement added for CS projects to install meters for future tenant spaces (min. 1 per floor).</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>

</ul>
