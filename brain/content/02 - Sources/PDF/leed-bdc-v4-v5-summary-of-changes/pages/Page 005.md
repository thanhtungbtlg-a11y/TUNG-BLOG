## Sustainable Sites

The Sustainable Sites (SS) credit category emphasizes the importance of sustainable site development by minimizing environmental impact while maximizing ecological benefits in site development, focusing on preserving natural habitats, promoting sustainable water management, enhancing climate resilience, and creating spaces that benefit both nature and people.

The credit category includes a new credit, SSc Enhanced Resilient Site Design, intended to reduce risk of catastrophic impacts from natural and climate events through resilient site design, construction, and maintenance.
