<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Enhanced Indoor Air Quality Strategies (continued)</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Increased ventilation incorporated into new credit: EQc Enhanced Air Quality.</li>
<li>Requirement changed to 15% increase in outdoor air ventilation for 1 point, and 30% increase for exemplary performance.</li>
<li>Monitoring incorporated into EQc Air Quality Testing and Monitoring.</li>
<li>Removed the following strategies: interior cross contamination prevention, natural ventilation design calculations, mixed-mode design calculations, exterior contamination prevention, additional source control and monitoring, and natural ventilation room-by-room calculations.</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Low-Emitting Materials</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Moved to the Materials and Resources category.</li>
<li>Reduced total point thresholds and modified the approach for awarding points.</li>
<li>Modified percentage compliance for paints/coatings, flooring, ceilings, adhesives and sealants, walls, insulation, composite wood and furniture.</li>
<li>Removed budget calculation method option.</li>
<li>Elaborated products and materials that can be excluded.</li>
<li>Added option to document paints and coatings by cost or surface area.</li>
<li>Added option to document adhesives and sealants by cost.</li>
<li>Reference standard for VOC emissions evaluation criteria updated to CDPH v1.2-2017.</li>
<li>Updated reference standard for furniture emissions evaluation criteria to ANSI/BIFMA Standard Method M7.1-2011(R2021) and ANSI/BIFMA e3-2014 or e3-2024 and elaborated on requirements for seating products, classroom furniture and appropriate evaluation scenarios.</li>
<li>Added requirements for formaldehyde emissions evaluation.</li>
<li>Removed additional VOC content requirements for wet-applied products.</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Construction Indoor Air Quality Management Plan</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Incorporated into new prerequisite: EQp Construction Management.</li>
<li>Added the following construction management practices: extreme heat protection, source control, pathway interruption, housekeeping &amp; scheduling.</li>
<li>Modified requirement for filtration during construction.</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credit</td>
<td><strong>Indoor Air Quality Assessment</strong></td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Incorporated into new credit: EQc Air Quality Testing and Monitoring</li>
<li>Modified requirements for air testing option and added a point for particulate matter (PM) and inorganic gases testing and added a separate point for volatile organic compounds (VOC) testing.</li>
<li>Updated reference standards and test methods and target compounds for VOC testing.</li>
<li>Added a new option for one point for continuous indoor air monitoring of CO2, PM2.5, TVOC, temperature, and relative humidity.</li>
<li>Removed flush out option.</li>
</ul>
</td>
