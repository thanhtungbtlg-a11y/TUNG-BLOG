## Water Efficiency

The Water Efficiency (WE) category integrates traditional efficiency measures with innovative stewardship strategies, redefining water as a valuable, limited resource. LEED v5 introduces a whole-project approach to water management, emphasizing alternative water sources, decarbonization through reduced energy use in water systems, and advanced metering for leak detection. By implementing high-performance requirements for appliances, encouraging real-time data monitoring, and promoting water conservation both on-site and in the wider community, LEED v5 addresses pressing challenges of water stress and scarcity. This comprehensive approach not only safeguards ecosystems and reduces carbon emissions but also contributes to improved quality of life and a more resilient, sustainable future.
