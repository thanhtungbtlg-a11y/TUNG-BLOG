## Materials and Resources

The Materials and Resources (MR) category focuses on reducing embodied carbon, protecting health, and fostering a circular economy. It emphasizes multi-attribute product selection, supply chain decarbonization, and industry alignment. Key strategies include whole building life cycle assessments, EPD analysis, waste management, and promoting low-emitting and circular materials. The category addresses embodied carbon reduction through manufacturing innovations and circular strategies, improves indoor environmental quality, and supports ecological conservation. By simplifying and harmonizing approaches, LEED v5 enables project teams to make high-impact choices that cut emissions, improve health outcomes, and advance sustainable practices throughout the building industry.
