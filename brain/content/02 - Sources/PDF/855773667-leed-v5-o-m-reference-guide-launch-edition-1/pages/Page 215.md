Through healthy building management practices, project teams can reduce their building’s
impact on surrounding environments by minimizing pollutants and pesticides ( _EQc7: Green_
_Cleaning_, _EQc8: Integrated Pest Management_ ). Environmentally sensitive pest control methods
promote long-term ecological balance.

EQ prerequisites and credits empower project owners, occupants, and the building community
to cultivate buildings in which people can experience a sense of belonging to their built
environment, community, and natural world.
