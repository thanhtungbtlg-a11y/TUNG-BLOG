**Calculation of project on-site combustion emissions for all Option 1 paths**
For projects where only one fuel is used for on-site combustion, such as natural gas, total
annual on-site combustion emissions are equal to the annual fuel consumption, multiplied by the
GHG emissions conversion factor for that fuel.

If multiple fuels are used in the project, sum together the total annual on-site combustion
emissions for each fuel.

Refer to the additional guidance in _Option 3. Total Greenhouse Gas Emissions from Building_
_Energy Use_ below addressing GHG emissions conversion factors for fuels.

**District heating systems**
For projects supplied with district heating, either refer to District Energy System (DES)
compliance paths in the Project Priorities Library or assign the combustion emissions
associated with district heating generation to the project.

Use the published district heating combustion emissions factor, if available, when assigning
combustion emissions. A default district heating on-site combustion GHG emissions factor of
66.4 kg/MBTU (227 g/kWh) may be used if this information is unavailable. District energy
