does not achieve the standard nonroof or roof calculations, teams may use an SRI and SR
weighted average approach to calculate compliance. The weighted nonroof or roof equation
weights the SR and SRI for total hardscape and roof area, showing its overall consequence on
heat island effect. This equation is useful for projects that have multiple roof angles and nonroof
or roof materials that fall both above and below the required SR and SRI values.
