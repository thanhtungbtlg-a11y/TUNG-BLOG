**Building energy efficiency**

  - Property types eligible to receive an ENERGY STAR® score in the U.S. and Canada
must comply with Option 1.

  - Property types eligible to receive an ENERGY STAR® score outside of the U.S. and
Canada must comply with either Option 1 or Option 2.

  - Property types referenced in Appendix II, Table 1 that are ineligible to receive an
ENERGY STAR® score must comply with either Option 2 or Option 3.

  - All other property types must comply with Option 3 or review additional
guidance/requirements.


**Option 1. ENERGY STAR® Score**

During the 12-month reporting period, achieve an ENERGY STAR® score of at least 60 using
the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) ENERGY STAR® Portfolio Manager [54] tool.

To apply Option 1 to projects outside of the U.S. and Canada, consult _ASHRAE_ _Standard 169-_
_2021_, “Climatic Data for Building Design Standards,” [55] and select an ENERGY STAR® Portfolio
Manager location in the same climate zone and with similar climate characteristics.
