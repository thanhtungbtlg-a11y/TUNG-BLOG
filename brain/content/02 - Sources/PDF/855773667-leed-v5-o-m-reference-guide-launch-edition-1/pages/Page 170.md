The following strategies support the achievement of these rates:


  - **Preventative maintenance** _._ Effective refrigerant management plans include
preventative maintenance considerations for all equipment. Regular inspections
occurring monthly for larger equipment or quarterly for smaller equipment will increase
the chances that minor leaks are corrected early, which leads to lower annual leakage
rates.

  - **Automatic leak detection** _._ Using automatic leak detection systems provides facility
teams with continuous monitoring of refrigerant-containing equipment. When alarms are
triggered, immediate corrective action for repair and refrigerant recharge reduces the
refrigerant leakage emissions ratio.
