**Report Energy Data**

The data must include 12 months of consecutive energy consumption from each energy source,
including on-site renewable energy generation. Projects must also commit to reporting this
monthly data on an annual basis following certification.

DATA ACCURACY

Teams must maintain accurate reporting, including an accurate representation of the total
energy consumed on site. Automating data collection will allow for efficiencies in reporting and
additional quality control and assurance during data entry.

Teams must confirm that all site energy use is accounted for within the reporting period. If the
reported energy use includes sub-metered data or renewable energy generated on-site or offsite, review the annual reported site energy use to confirm that this energy is neither double
counted nor excluded from total usage except where specifically allowed.
