**Option 1. Greenhouse Gas Emissions from On-Site Combustion (1–5 points)**

Demonstrate a percentage improvement in on-site combustion emissions below the on-site
combustion emissions target based on the project energy data reported in _EAp3: Minimum_
_Energy Performance_ . Points are awarded according to Table 1.

Projects that neither use on-site combustion except for emergency support systems (used less
than 200 hours per year) nor use district heating automatically achieve five points.

Determine the on-site combustion emissions target using any applicable path.
