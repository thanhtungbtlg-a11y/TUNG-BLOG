CALCULATION FOR REDUCTION AND REUSE

There are two methods for calculating potential diversion numbers through the reduction and
reuse of materials in the project site’s material streams.

**Calculating reduction**
Many projects find ways to reduce the amount of materials being purchased and generated by
implementing more efficient practices within the project boundary. When a project switches to a
new strategy, like a reusable container, and prevents the generation of material in the process,
then this action would be documented under the Reduction category in your diversion data
tracking system. The weights of materials not generated due to a reduction action are tracked
and used in calculations in the same way as the weights for actual materials. [89]


  - Measure the weight of the object that was used and disposed of before switching to the
new waste prevention strategy.

  - Multiply the weight of that object by the number of objects no longer being purchased or
discarded.

  - Subtract the current weight total of the object from the weight total that is no longer being
purchased to get the reduction.

Reduction weights can continue to be counted every year regardless of when the initial
reduction event took place as long as the original reduction strategy is still in place and being
implemented.
