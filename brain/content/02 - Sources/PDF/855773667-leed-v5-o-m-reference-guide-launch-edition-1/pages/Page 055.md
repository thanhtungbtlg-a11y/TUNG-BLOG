VENTILATION SYSTEM PREVENTATIVE MAINTENANCE

For the project’s ventilation systems, include all applicable inspection/maintenance tasks from
_ASHRAE 62.1-2022_, Table 8-1 Minimum Maintenance Activity and Frequency for Ventilation
System Equipment and Associated Components. [18] Ensure the project’s preventative
maintenance plan references at least the minimum frequency required in Table 8-1.
