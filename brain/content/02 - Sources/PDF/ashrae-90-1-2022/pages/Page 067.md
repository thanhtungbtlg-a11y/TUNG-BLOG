**5.6 Building Envelope Trade-Off Compliance Path**
**5.6.1** The _building envelope_ complies with the standard if


a. the _proposed_ _design_ satisfies the provisions of Sections 5.1, 5.4, 5.7, 5.8, and 5.9 and
b. the _proposed envelope performance factor_ of the _proposed design_ is less than or equal to the _proposed_

_envelope performance factor_ of the _base design_ .
