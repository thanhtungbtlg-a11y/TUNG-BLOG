**7.8 Product Information**

**7.9 Verification, Testing, and Commissioning**
**7.9.1 Verification and Testing.** Service hot-water controls shall be verified and tested in accordance with
this section and Section 4.2.5.1. Testing shall verify that _systems_ and controls are configured and operating
in accordance with applicable requirements of


a. _service water heating system_ temperature controls (Sections 7.4.4.1 and 7.4.4.3),
b. recirculation _pump_ or _heat trace_ controls (Section 7.4.4.2), or
c. _pool_ time switch controls (Section 7.4.5.3).


Verification and _FPT_ documentation shall comply with Section 4.2.5.1.2.
**7.9.2 Commissioning.** The _energy_ performance of the _service water heating systems_ shall be _commis-_
_sioned_ in accordance with Section 4.2.5.2, and reporting shall comply with Section 4.2.5.2.2.
