**Table 9.3.2 Simplified Building Method for Building Exteriors**

|Base allowance|200 W|_Luminaires_ shall be turned off or the power reduced by a minimum of 75% during<br>nonoperating hours.|

|Stairs|Exempt|No additional controls required.|

a. To calculate the exterior allowance, multiply the _space_ or area square footage by the allowed W/ft [2] and sum the exterior allowances and the base allowance. Façade lighting shall

be calculated separately by multiplying the facade area by the allowed W/ft [2] . Façade allowance shall not be traded with other exterior areas or between separate _facade areas_ .
b. For _buildings_ in Lighting Zone 2, as defined in Table 9.4.2-1, multiply exterior allowances by 0.7. For _buildings_ in Lighting Zone 4, as defined in Table 9.4.2-1, multiply exterior

allowances by 1.4.
