c. The cooling-mode wet bulb temperature requirement only applies for units that reject condensate to the condenser coil.

e. “Cap” in _EER_ and _COPH_ equations for _PTAC_ s and _PTHP_ s means cooling capacity in Btu/h at 95°F outdoor dry-bulb temperature.
