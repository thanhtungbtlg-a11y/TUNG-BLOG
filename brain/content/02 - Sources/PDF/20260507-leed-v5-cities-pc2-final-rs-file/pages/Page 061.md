###### **TM CREDIT: SAFE AND AFFORDABLE PUBLIC TRANSIT SERVICES**

**TMc2**


1–2 points


**Intent**

To encourage improvement in public transit systems and operations to ensure better accessibility, equity,

and safety of all transit users.


**Requirements**

|Achievement Pathways Points|Col2|
|---|---|
|Cities<br>|1–2<br>|
|Option 1. Transit Efficiency<br>|2 <br>|
|OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 2. Transportation Equity<br>|1 <br>|
|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 3. Transportation Safety<br>|1|



Comply with any combination of the following options for a maximum of 2 points.


**Option 1. Transit Efficiency (2 points)**

Demonstrate at least 60% of the population resides within a ½-mile (800-meter) radius of high-frequency

transit or a ¼-mile (400-meter) radius of transit stops.


AND


Demonstrate a 5% improvement in the public transport services for one of the following metrics from a

user-defined baseline year. The selected baseline year must fall within the last five years:

a. Annual ridership per capita

b. Number of lines/routes

c. Miles of network length per million population

d. Number of vehicles per million population
