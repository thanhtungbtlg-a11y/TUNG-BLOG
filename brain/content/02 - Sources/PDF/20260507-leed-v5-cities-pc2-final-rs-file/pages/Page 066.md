**AND/OR**


**Option 2. Parking Management (1 point)**

Demonstrate that city-wide parking policies exist that include three of the following strategies:

a. **Preferential Parking:** Provide designated parking spaces for rideshare (car/van pool) vehicles.

b. **Parking Maximums:** Regulate the amount of parking capacity allowed in all land-use zones

within the city using parking maximums.

c. **Eliminate Parking Minimums:** Adopt city-wide policy to remove minimum off-street parking

requirement for all land uses.

d. **Shared Parking:** Allow mixed-use development or shared facilities to reduce total parking

requirements by demonstrating that the peak parking demand for specific land uses can be

accommodated at different times (daily or seasonally).

e. **Unbundled Parking:** Require that parking spaces be sold or rented separately from building

purchases and leases in a defined area, such as a residential complex or a commercial district,

where parking is managed separately.

f. **Dynamic Parking Rates:** Set on-street parking rates dynamically by increasing prices during

peak demand periods and decreasing them during off-peak times, with the objective of

maintaining an average parking occupancy of 80% to 85%.


**AND/OR**


**Option 3. Advanced Mobility Solutions (1 point)**

Adopt four of the following city-wide policies or solutions to facilitate a smart and efficient transportation

system:


a. **Passenger Information System (PIS):** 80% of all transit stations and transit stops within the city

are equipped with a PIS system.

b. **Automated Speed Enforcement:** 80% of roads are equipped for automated speed enforcement;

alleyways and roads not controlled by municipality/civic bodies are exempted.

c. **Traffic Surveillance** : 80% of all transit stations and transit stops within the city are equipped with

CCTVs for traffic surveillance.

d. **Global Positioning System (GPS)/General Packet Radio Service (GPRS):** All public transit

vehicles are equipped with a GPS/GPRS system.

e. **Signal Synchronization and Transit Signal Priority:** All signals on arterial roads are

synchronized or prioritized to address varying traffic flows.
