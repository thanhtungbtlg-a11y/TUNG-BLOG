**Option 1. Reduction of Exposure Over Time (2 points)**

Identify at least one _priority neighborhood_ from _IPp2 Human Impact Assessment_ that is affected by a

strategies listed below.


_a._ Implement structural and/or engineered solutions in compliance with national, state, or local

standards to reduce the impact from at least one _prioritized risk_ identified in _IPp4: Resilience_

_Assessment._

include but are not limited to focus on the location of critical facilities, protective setbacks, and

development standards/restrictions.

c. Incorporate structure resilience requirements in building regulations to reduce potential damage

from identified _prioritized risks_ .
