###### **WR CREDIT: CIVIC LEADERSHIP—PROCUREMENT AND RESILIENCE**

**WRc5**


1–2 points


**Intent**

To advance municipal sustainable procurement practices that prioritize low-carbon, reused, and

repurposed materials while strengthening city-wide waste service resilience.


**Requirements**

|Achievement Pathways Points|Col2|
|---|---|
|Cities<br>|1–2<br>|

|AND/OR<br>|<br>|

|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 3. Waste Systems Resilience<br>|1|



Comply with any combination of the following options for a maximum of 2 points.


**Option 1. Sustainable Construction Procurement Policy (1 point)**

Demonstrate that the city has adopted a sustainable construction procurement policy applicable to city
owned construction, major renovation, and infrastructure projects with a construction budget exceeding

$1M (USD). The policy must include one of the following provisions:


a. Allocate ≥5% of the total materials budget to reused/reclaimed products and/or materials.

b. Allocate ≥10% of the total materials budget to products with third-party-verified EPDs that

demonstrate below industry average embodied carbon for their product category.

c. Include completion of a whole-building life cycle assessment (WBLCA) as part of the project

scope and budget, consistent with _LEED BD+C v5 MRc2 Reduce Embodied Carbon._


AND


Commit to publishing an annual report that summarizes the city’s sustainable construction purchasing

activities and includes quantified impact metrics demonstrating outputs and outcomes.
