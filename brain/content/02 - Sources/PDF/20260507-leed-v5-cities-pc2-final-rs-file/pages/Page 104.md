**Option 2. Material-Focused Codes (1 point)**

Adopt and implement a city-wide policy or amendment to the building code that supports either

prescriptive or performance-based embodied carbon reductions in buildings and/or infrastructure projects.

The policy or amendment must include whole building life-cycle assessments (WBLCA) for projects or

establish embodied carbon thresholds for at least one of the following materials: concrete, steel, glass

(glazing), wood, and asphalt.


AND


Commit to publishing an annual report that summarizes the embodied carbon reduction initiatives and

includes quantified impact metrics demonstrating outputs and outcomes.


**AND/OR**


**Option 3. Material Recovery (1–2 points)**

Adopt and implement a city-wide policy or program to incentivize building deconstruction and salvage.

Meet one of the following requirements to earn 1 point or meet three to earn 2 points:

a. Require a salvage assessment prior to the issuance of demolition permits.

b. Include incentives such as grants or expedited permit reviews for whole-building deconstruction.

c. Implement a demolition notification and delay requirement that provides public notice and a

minimum 35-day waiting period before permit issuance.

d. Adopt and implement a deconstruction requirement for applicable projects that prioritizes

disassembly and salvage of materials with established or emerging regional resale/collection

markets.

e. Adopt and implement an incentive program for new construction that requires a minimum amount

of material reuse.


AND


Commit to publishing an annual report that summarizes the deconstruction and salvage-related initiatives

and includes quantified impact metrics demonstrating outputs and outcomes.
