**Option 2. Access to Bicycle Facilities (2 points)**

Provide short-term bicycle storage within 600 feet (180 meters) and long-term bicycle storage and shower

facilities within 300 feet (90 meters) of walking distance from any functional entry for 75% of all city
occupied buildings per Table 1.

Long-term bicycle parking and shower facilities shall not be required where the total building floor area is

less than 2,500 square feet or 232 square meters and has less than 100 regular building occupants.
