**Intent**

improve overall travel experience through a people-centric and user-friendly transportation system.

Comply with any combination of the following options for a maximum of 2 points.


**Option 1. Transportation Demand Management (TDM) Policy (1 point)**

Establish a city-wide TDM policy designating a transportation management coordinator and requiring

major employers to participate in city-wide TDM efforts, either through a transportation management

association or a city-managed program.


AND


Adopt three of the following TDM strategies to reduce single-occupant vehicle trips available for TMA

members:

a. Commuter financial incentives (parking cashout and transit allowance)

b. Telecommuting incentives

c. Alternate scheduling (flextime, compressed work weeks)

d. Car pool/van pool program and ride matching

e. Enhanced pedestrian and bicycle facilities

f. Shuttles from transit stations

g. Proximity commuting

h. Guaranteed ride home program
