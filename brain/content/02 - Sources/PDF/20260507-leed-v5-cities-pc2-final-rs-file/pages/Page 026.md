###### **NB CREDIT: HEAT ISLAND REDUCTION**

**NBc2**


1–2 points


**Intent**

To minimize heat island and extreme heat impacts on microclimates and habitats and improve thermal

comfort, air quality, and energy efficiency for a healthier and more livable city.


**Requirements**

|Achievement Pathways Points|Col2|
|---|---|
|Cities<br>|1–2<br>|
|Option 1. City-Wide Heat Island Reduction<br>|1–2<br>|
|Path 1. Performance Pathway<br>|2 <br>|
|OR<br>|<br>|

|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 2. Heat Island Reduction Policies<br>|1|



Comply with any of the following options for a maximum of 2 points.


**Option 1. City-Wide Heat Island Reduction (2 points)**


PATH 1. PERFORMANCE PATHWAY (2 POINTS):


Demonstrate at least 5% improvement in one of the following the city-wide heat island reduction

strategies in the reporting year from a user-defined baseline year. The selected baseline year

must fall within the last five years. Use any of the following strategies to show improvement:

a. Increased tree canopy cover within the city

b. Increased natural resource acreage within the city

c. Reduction in surface and/or air temperature of the city

d. Increase in the albedo mapping of the city


Natural resource areas include, but are not limited to, undeveloped and uninhabited topographic

features, soils, terrestrial vegetation, terrestrial habitat, hydrology, and aquatic ecosystems as

identified in _IPp1: Ecosystem Assessment_ . Green spaces as defined in _NBc5: Accessible and_

_Inclusive Open Spaces_ can be included within the natural resource area calculation.
