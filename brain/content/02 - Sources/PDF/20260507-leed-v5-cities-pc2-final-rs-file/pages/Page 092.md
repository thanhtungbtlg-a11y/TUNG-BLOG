###### **EC CREDIT: ENERGY-EFFICIENT CIVIC BUILDINGS**

**ECc4**


1–3 points


**Intent**

To improve the energy efficiency of city-occupied buildings to reduce the environmental harm and lower

the city’s carbon footprint.


**Requirements**

|Achievement Pathways Points|Col2|
|---|---|
|Cities<br>|1–3<br>|
|Option 1. Energy and Carbon Tracking<br>|1 <br>|
|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 2. Energy Audit<br>|1 <br>|
|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 3. Existing Building Commissioning<br>|1 <br>|
|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 4. Strategic Decarbonization Plan<br>|1 <br>|
|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 5. Energy Efficiency<br>|2–3|



Comply with any combination of the following options for a maximum of 3 points.


**Option 1. Energy and Carbon Tracking (1 point)**

Track, benchmark, and report energy consumption and carbon emissions (electricity, natural gas, chilled

water, steam or hot water, fuel oil, propane) of city-occupied buildings at intervals of at least once per

month.

Include 75% of the total gross floor area of city-occupied buildings that are 10,000 square feet (929

square meters) or larger. Report data by energy source for the most recent reporting year.


**AND/OR**


**Option 2. Energy Audit (1 point)**

Conduct energy audits for 10% of the total gross floor area of city-occupied buildings that are 25,000

square feet (2,323 square meters) or larger.
