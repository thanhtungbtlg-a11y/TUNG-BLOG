**WRc4**


1 point


**Intent**

To recover high-value materials from the waste stream and enable their reuse, repair, remanufacture,

repurpose, and collection to advance a circular economy.

**Option 1. Repair/Reuse Awareness and Education (1 point)**

Collaborate with nonprofit, trades, and commercial partners to meet two of the following requirements:


a. Launch an awareness and education campaign around reuse, repair, and reimagining waste.

b. Maintain a network of skilled volunteers who can repair electronics and appliances and

provide clothing-alteration services.

c. Conduct at least two repair-related training programs each year, reaching at least 200

participants in total annually.

d. Maintain one program that accepts household items for repair/refurbishment and returns

them to use through resale or redistribution.


AND


Commit to publishing an annual report that summarizes the repair/reuse initiatives and includes quantified

impact metrics demonstrating outputs and outcomes.
