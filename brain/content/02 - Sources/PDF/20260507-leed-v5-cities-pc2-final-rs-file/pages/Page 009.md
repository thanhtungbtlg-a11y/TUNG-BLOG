|Human Impact Assessment<br>||



Conduct a city-wide Human Impact Assessment to identify inequities in quality of life and public health

outcomes and the underlying drivers of those inequities. Include information about the following topics as

applicable. Identify _priority neighborhoods_ with the highest cumulative needs and priority populations

experiencing disproportionate impacts.


**1. Land Use and Population Profile:**

a. **Demographics:** race and ethnicity, gender, age, population density, income, employment

rate, education levels, household types, unhoused and housed population, health status, and

crime rates

b. **Local Development Patterns:** land use development, zoning and development history, and

relevant local or regional sustainability goals/commitments


**2. Distribution of Services:**

a. **Utility Services and Infrastructure:**




`o` Drinking water supply and sanitary wastewater services




`o` Grid or on-site renewable electricity sources

`o` Municipal or contracted solid waste services



b. **Healthcare and Social Infrastructure:** hospitals/health services, parks, libraries,



recreation centers, schools, childcare centers, fire stations, police stations, internet

(broadband) connectivity, and supermarket/grocery stores with a produce section
