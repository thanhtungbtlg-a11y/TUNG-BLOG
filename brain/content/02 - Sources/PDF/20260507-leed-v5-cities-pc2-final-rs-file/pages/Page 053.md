**Intent**

To promote a diverse and skilled workforce by providing expanded educational and training opportunities

that enhance employment readiness.


**Requirements**

|Achievement Pathways Points|Col2|
|---|---|
|Cities<br>|1–2<br>|
|Option 1. Postsecondary Education<br>|1 <br>|
|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 2. Employment Distribution<br>|2 <br>|
|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 3. Career Assistance and Support Programs<br>|1|



Comply with any combination of the following options for a maximum of 2 points.


**Option 1. Postsecondary Education (1 point)**

Demonstrate that the postsecondary educational attainment of the city population aged 25 years and

older in any two _priority neighborhoods_ identified in _IPp2: Human Impact Assessment_ is within five

percentage points of the overall city average. Provide data for a year that is within the last five years.


**AND/OR**


**Option 2. Employment Distribution (2 Points)**

Meet the following requirements:

a. Demonstrate that the overall unemployment rate is trending toward or has reached the national

definition of full employment.

b. Demonstrate that the unemployment rate in any two priority neighborhoods and/or priority

populations identified in _IPp2: Human Impact Assessment_ is within five percentage points of the

overall city unemployment rate.

Provide data for a year that is within the last five years.
