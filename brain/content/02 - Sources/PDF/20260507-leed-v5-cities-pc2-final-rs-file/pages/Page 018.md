###### **IP CREDIT: CLIMATE ACTION PLAN**

**IPc3**


1 point


**Intent**

To encourage cities to outline measurable strategies that accelerate the reduction of carbon emissions

and build climate resilience in sectors projected to be impacted by climate hazards.


**Requirements**

|Achievement Pathways Points|Col2|
|---|---|
|Cities<br>|1 <br>|
|Climate Action Plan<br>|1|



Develop a Climate Action Plan to accelerate the reduction of carbon emissions and achieve at least 80%

city-wide carbon emissions reduction by 2050. The plan must meet the following requirements:


1. **Stakeholder Engagement:** Constitute a diverse and inclusive group of key parties, such as

residents, businesses and/or institutions, municipal and state officials, technical experts or

advisory groups, representatives of vulnerable populations and indigenous groups, to gather

insights and feedback.

2. **Vision and Objectives:**

a. Geographic and/or administrative boundary of the city

b. Carbon emission reduction target with interim and short-term goals

c. City-wide carbon emission inventory for the base year (must include Scope 1 and Scope 2;

Scope 3 is optional) with breakdown by sector and the business-as-usual projection of city
wide carbon emissions through 2050.

3. I **mplementation and Monitoring:**

a. Outline initiatives for each sector and ensure that each initiative addresses the following

points:




`o` Implementation timeline




`o` Estimated impact on carbon emissions




`o` Implementation processes, such as passing an ordinance, creating a city-wide policy,



funding a program, planning, and working with the state or province




`o` Estimated budget and/or required staffing

b. Method for monitoring, evaluating, and reporting the impacts of the initiatives
