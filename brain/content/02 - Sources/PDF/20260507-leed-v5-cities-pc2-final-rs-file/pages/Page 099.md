**Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) Generation**

Report the total weight of MSW (in tonnes per capita) generated in 12 consecutive months within the last

five years.

MSW generated must include waste generation from all sectors within the city, including but not limited to

residential, institutional, commercial, any other sectors, and open spaces.


**AND**


**Waste Diversion**

Report the total weight of the MSW that is diverted (as a percentage of total MSW generation) from

landfills or incineration (including waste to energy) for the same 12 consecutive months as MSW

generation data.
