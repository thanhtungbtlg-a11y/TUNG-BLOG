###### **IP CREDIT: ENHANCED RESILIENCE**

**IPc5**


1–3 points


**Intent**

To reduce loss of life, livelihood, and assets; strengthen recovery and reconstruction preparedness; build

resilience to _prioritized risks_ ; and support the city’s long-term public health and economic vitality.


**Requirements**

|Achievement Pathways Points|Col2|
|---|---|
|Cities<br>|1–3<br>|
|Option 1. Risk Mitigation<br>|1–2<br>|
|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 2. Preparedness<br>|1–2|



Comply with any combination of the following options for a maximum of 2 points.


**Option 1. Risk Mitigation (1–2 points)**

Demonstrate that risk has been reduced to at least one at-risk population, building(s), critical

infrastructure, or ecosystem identified under _IPp4: Resilience Assessment_ by adopting any of following

the strategies for 1 point each:


a. Implement nature-based and/or engineering solutions in compliance with national, state, or local

standards to reduce at least one _prioritized risk_ as identified under _IPp4: Resilience Assessment._

b. Adopt policies on risk-informed zoning and/or limitation of construction in vulnerable zones and/or

protective setbacks.

c. Incorporate building structure resilience in building regulations to enhance the structural resilience

of new and/or existing buildings to the _prioritized risks_ .

d. Support voluntary relocation of at-risk population and/or critical infrastructure away from hazard
prone zones. Ensure adequate infrastructure in destination areas to support relocated

populations.
