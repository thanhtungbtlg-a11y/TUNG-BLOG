**Option 1. Provision of Open Spaces (1–2 points)**


PATH 1. ALLOCATION OF PUBLIC GREEN SPACES (1 POINT):

Provide publicly accessible green space area covering 15% or more of the total city area.


AND/OR


PATH 2. OPEN RECREATIONAL SPACES (1 POINT)

Provide two of the following spaces within a ½-mile (800-meter) buffer of at least 70% of the city’s

residential areas:

    - Play space geared toward children

    - Co-located adult and child physical activity space
