#### **PROJECT PRIORITIES (PR)**

###### **PROJECT PRIORITIES**

**PRc1**


1–10 points


**Intent**

To promote achievement of credits that address regional environmental, social equity, and public health

priorities and to encourage cities to achieve exceptional or innovative performance.


**Requirements**

|Achievement Pathways Points|Col2|
|---|---|
|Cities<br>|1–10<br>|
|**Regional Priority** <br>|1–9<br>|
|Exemplary Performance<br>|1–9<br>|
|Pilot Credits<br>|1–9<br>|
|Innovative Strategies<br>|1–9<br>|
|LEED Credentials<br>|1|



Achieve any combination of the following for a maximum of 10 points. Each option/strategy is worth 1

point.


**Regional Priority**

Demonstrate achievement of a credit (part of the main seven credit categories) that enables the project to

achieve significant, measurable environmental, and/or social performance for a regional priority.


The project must achieve at least half the maximum points (or at least 1 point if the maximum available

point in the credit is 1) available in the credit identified as regional priority to be eligible to achieve this

credit.


**Exemplary Performance**

Achieve an exemplary performance credit from USGBC’s Project Priority Library. These credits have

been identified by USGBC as going above and beyond an existing LEED v5 Cities credit in the LEED v5

priority areas of scale, decarbonization, resilience, health, equity, and/or ecosystems.
