###### **CW CREDIT: EDUCATIONAL OPPORTUNITY AND ATTAINMENT**

**CWc5**


1–2 points


**Intent**

To achieve equitable attainment of quality education from birth to adulthood and increase opportunities to

enter the workforce upon graduation.


**Requirements**

|Achievement Pathways Points|Col2|
|---|---|
|Cities<br>|1–2<br>|
|Option 1. Early Learning Proficiency<br>|2 <br>|
|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 2. High School Education<br>|2 <br>|
|AND/OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 3. Support Programs to Improve Retention and Improving Graduation Rates<br>|1|



Comply with any combination of the following options for a maximum of 2 points.


**Option 1. Early Learning Proficiency (2 points)**

Meet the following requirements:

a. Demonstrate that at least 85% of third-grade public school students meet minimum

proficiency scores in mathematics or reading.

b. Demonstrate that the corresponding score in any two _priority neighborhoods_ identified in

_IPp2: Human Impact Assessment_ is within five percentage points of the city-wide third-grade

public school proficiency score for the same subject.

Provide data for a year that is within the last five years.


**AND/OR**


**Option 2. High School Education (2 points)**

Meet the following requirements:

a. Demonstrate achievement of a 90% average four-year adjusted cohort high school

graduation rate for all students in all schools.
