## Extracted Content

Adopt city-wide policies to allocate dedicated funding for public transit improvements in any two _priority_

_neighborhoods_ identified in _IPp2: Human Impact Assessment_ to ensure equitable access to efficient and

affordable transportation options.


AND


Provide one of the following benefits to achieve affordable fare structure for commuters with low income:


a. **Concession Fares:** Offer reduced fares for children, students, seniors, and/or persons with

disability.

b. **Subscription Passes:** Provide discounted weekly, monthly, or multitrip passes for commuters.

c. **Fare Capping:** Implement a daily or monthly fare cap to limit the total cost of travel for regular

commuters.

d. **Fare Compatibility:** Enable fare payment across multiple modes for intermodal journeys using

the same payment medium.


**AND/OR**


**Option 3. Transportation Safety (1 point)**

Meet the following requirements:

a. Demonstrate a 10% total reduction in pedestrian and bicyclist fatalities and severe injuries from a

user-defined baseline year. The selected baseline year must fall within the last five years.

b. Demonstrate a 10% total reduction in vehicular fatalities and severe injuries from a user-defined

baseline year. The selected baseline year must fall within the last five years.


AND


Adopt city-wide Vision Zero strategies to eliminate traffic fatalities and severe injuries and increase

healthy and equitable mobility for all.
