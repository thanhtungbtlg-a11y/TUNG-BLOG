###### **WR CREDIT: EMBODIED CARBON REDUCTION**

**WRc3**


1–2 points


**Intent**

To strengthen the approach to purchasing, preserving, and recovering building materials to

environmentally, economically, and socially preferable life-cycle impacts.


**Requirements**

|Achievement Pathways Points|Col2|
|---|---|
|Cities<br>|2 <br>|
|Option 1. Adaptive Reuse<br>|1 <br>|
|OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 2. Material-Focused Codes<br>|1 <br>|
|OR<br>|<br>|
|Option 3. Material Recovery<br>|1–2|



Comply with any of the following options for a total of up to 2 points.


**Option 1. Adaptive Reuse (1 point)**


Implement an awareness and outreach strategy/policy to promote the adaptive reuse of existing

buildings. Include the following:

a. Awareness of embodied carbon benefits of reuse compared with demolition and new construction

b. Guidance on change of use/occupancy and applicable code pathways

c. Awareness about available zoning/design/permitting flexibility

d. Training/outreach for key stakeholders, such as owners, developers, design professionals, and

permitting staff


AND


Provide financial incentives and/or establish processes to advance building reuse and adaptation, such

as expedited permit review, dedicated permit navigation support, streamlined permitting, or development

fee reductions/waivers.
