Indoor Environmental Quality Credit
### **ENHANCED AIR QUALITY**


**EQc1**
New Construction (1 point)
Core and Shell (1 point)

### **INTENT**





To design for increased indoor air quality (IAQ) to better protect the health of building occupants.

### **REQUIREMENTS**

|Achievement Pathways|Points|
|---|---|
|<br>New Construction and Core and Shell<br>|<br>1 <br>|
|<br>Option 1. Increased Ventilation<br>|<br>1 <br>|
|<br>OR<br>|<br> <br>|
|<br>Option 2. Enhanced Indoor Air Quality Design<br>|<br>1|



Design the building to exceed the requirements of _ASHRAE 62.1-2022_, Section 6. If using the
VRP to comply with _EQp2: Fundamental Air Quality_, use Option 1 or Option 2; if using the IAQP,
use Option 2.


**Option 1. Increased Ventilation (1 point)**

Increase breathing zone outdoor air ventilation rates by at least 15% above the minimum rates
(for 1 point, or 30% for exemplary performance) as determined in _EQp2: Fundamental Air_
_Quality_ .

Increased outdoor air rates should be provided to 95% of all regularly occupied spaces.


**OR**


**Option 2. Enhanced Indoor Air Quality Design (1 point)**

In addition to the design compounds and design limits outlined in _ASHRAE 62.1-2022_, Tables 65 and 6-6, design and verify enhanced IAQ using the lower design limits listed below in Table 1.
