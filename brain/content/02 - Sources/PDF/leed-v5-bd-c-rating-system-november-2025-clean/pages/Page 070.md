**Option 3. Appliance and Process Water (1–2 points)**

Newly installed equipment within the project boundary must meet the minimum requirements in
Tables 3, 4, 5, and/or 6. One point is awarded for meeting all applicable requirements in any 1
table for a maximum of 2 points. All applicable, newly installed equipment listed in each table
must meet the standard. Existing appliances and equipment can be excluded.

To use Table 3, the project must process at least 120,000 lb (57,606 kg) of laundry per year.
