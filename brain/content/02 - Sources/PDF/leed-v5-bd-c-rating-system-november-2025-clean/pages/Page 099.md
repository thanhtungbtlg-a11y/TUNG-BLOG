5.5, Prescriptive Building Envelope Compliance Path or _ASHRAE 90.1_, Section 5.6, Building
Envelope Trade-Off Compliance Path per the version of _ASHRAE 90.1_ referenced in _EAp2:_
_Minimum Energy Efficiency_ . Building envelope efficiency shall not be traded off with other
building systems.
