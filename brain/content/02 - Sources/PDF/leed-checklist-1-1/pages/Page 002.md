### LEED V4 RATING SYSTEM SELECTION GUIDANCE

Now that you know your facility meets the minimum requirements for LEED certification, it’s time to decipher
which classification you fall under. Use this guide to assist in your planning.


**[Source: http://www.usgbc.org/credits/existing-buildings/v4/minimum-program-requirements](http://www.usgbc.org/credits/existing-buildings/v4/minimum)**
