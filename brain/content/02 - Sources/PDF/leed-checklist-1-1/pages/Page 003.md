|**CHOOSING BETWEEN RATING SYSTEMS**|**CHOOSING BETWEEN RATING SYSTEMS**|
|**Step 1:**|First, assign a rating system to each square foot or square meter of your building.|
|**Step 2:**|Calculate the percentage of your facility that meets each rating system.|
|**Step 3:**|Choose the most appropriate rating system based on the resulting percentages.|
|||
|**Rule 1:**|Less than 40% of the gross foor area should not be used.|
|**Rule 2:**|Greater than 60% of the gross foor area should be used.|
|**Rule 3:**|Percentages between 40% and 60% of the gross foor area, then independently<br>assess each situation and decide which rating system is most applicable.|
