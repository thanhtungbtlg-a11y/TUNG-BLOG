ii. Online Portal


LEED certification is offered through an online portal and is the primary vehicle for managing the LEED documentation
process. Through the online portal, project teams can manage project details, complete documentation requirements for LEED
credits and prerequisites, upload supporting files, submit applications for review, receive reviewer feedback, and ultimately
earn LEED certification. The online portal provides a common space where members of a project team can work together to
document compliance with the LEED rating system.
