## Extracted Content

Appeals Panel is final. If the Board of Directors hears the appeal, its decision is final.


The Appeals Panel has authority to impose any remedy supported by and necessitated by the evidence presented, except that it
shall impose the most narrowly tailored remedy sufficient to resolve the appeal. By way of example, appeals related to a single
credit or policy, for example, may impose a remedy specific to that single credit or policy, but may not extend beyond the single
credit or policy and may require a re- balloting of such credit or policy.
