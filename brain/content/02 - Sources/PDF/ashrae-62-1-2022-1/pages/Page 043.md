**B2.3 Concentration Method.** Determine the acceptable concentration for health ( _Chealth_ ) and odor ( _Codor_ )
for each emitted chemical, compound, or mixture.

Design the exhaust and intake systems such that the maximum concentration at the intake ( _Cmax_ ) is less
than the acceptable concentrations of all evaluated compounds and mixtures.


_Cmax_ < _Chealth_ (B-4)


_Cmax_ < _Codor_ (B-5)


At a minimum, determination of _Cmax_ shall consider wind speed, wind direction, exhaust exit velocity
and momentum, geometry of building and adjacent structures, and architectural screens. Wind tunnel modeling is an acceptable design method.
