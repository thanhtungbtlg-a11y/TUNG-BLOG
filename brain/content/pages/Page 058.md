## OCR Text

The first three sections of this guide set a foundation for green
building practice by addressing integrated processes versus
conventional practice. This section builds on that groundwork,
presenting fundamental concepts alongside strategies for putting
green building into action.
Although there are many ways to organize green building projects, this section uses some of the major
categories associated with the LEED rating systems for organization:
LOCATION AND TRANSPORTATION
SUSTAINABLE SITES
WATER EFFICIENCY
ENERGY AND ATMOSPHERE
MATERIALS AND RESOURCES
INDOOR ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY
INNOVATION
Despite this organizational framework, many synergistic opportunities can be found both within and
between categories. For example, the location of a project can have a significant effect on occupants'
transportation choices, the project's energy needs, and potential opportunities for using renewable energy.
