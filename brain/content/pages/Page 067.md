To mitigate those harmful effects, project teams can install surfaces that have high solar reflectance (SR)
or solar reflectivity index (SRI). SRI combines reflectivity with emissivity, or the ability of a material to emit
energy through radiation. The use of reflective materials and those with high SRI values reduces heat gain,
thus increasing comfort and reducing demand for air-conditioning. Materials that help reduce the heat
island effect include open grid paving, white roofs, and vegetated "green" roofs.
STRATEGIES FOR REDUCING THE HEAT ISLAND EFFECT:
• INSTALL REFLECTIVE ROOF SURFACES. Light-colored roofs absorb less heat.

• REDUCE THE AREA OF PAVED SURFACES EXPOSED TO SUNLIGHT. Limit

hardscape with greenery, and locate parking underground.
• PLANT AN URBAN FOREST OR A GREEN ROOF. Use street trees, shrubs.
