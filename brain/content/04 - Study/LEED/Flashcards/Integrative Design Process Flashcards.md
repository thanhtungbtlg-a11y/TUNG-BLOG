# Integrative Design Process Flashcards

Source concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Integrative Design Process]]

## Card 1 - Requirement distinction

What separates the formal LEED v5 BD+C Integrative Design Process credit from general interdisciplinary coordination?
?
The formal credit requires an integrated team, facilitated early charrette, and measurable impact-area goals incorporated into the owner's project requirements. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 019|BD+C p. 19]] Concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Integrative Design Process]].

## Card 2 - Engineering application

Before MEP systems are fixed, how should an integrative team use the owner's project requirements?
?
**Vault-authored engineering application:** Translate the project's impact goals into coordinated criteria for envelope assumptions, HVAC loads and zoning, water demand, controls, metering, and commissioning, then record tradeoffs that can be tested later. [[02 - Sources/PDF/855773644-leed-v5-bd-c-reference-guide-launch-edition/pages/Page 050|BD+C guide p. 50]] Concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Integrative Design Process]].
