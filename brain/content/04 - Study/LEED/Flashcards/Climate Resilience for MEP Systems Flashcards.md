# Climate Resilience for MEP Systems Flashcards

Source concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Climate Resilience for MEP Systems]]

## Card 1 - Rating-system comparison

What is the common starting point for BD+C and O+M climate resilience, and how do their outcomes differ?
?
Both begin with a hazard assessment and prioritization. BD+C uses the result to inform design, construction, and operation, while O+M uses it to shape operations, maintenance, and emergency response. ## Card 2 - Engineering application

After a priority hazard is selected, which MEP dependencies should be mapped before choosing a resilience strategy?
?
**Vault-authored engineering application:** Map utilities, plant, distribution, controls, occupied zones, critical loads, operators, and external services so a strategy addresses the full service chain rather than a single component.
