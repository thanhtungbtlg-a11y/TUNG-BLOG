# Thermal Comfort Flashcards

Source concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Thermal Comfort]]

## Card 1 - Definition recall

Why is a thermostat setpoint alone insufficient to describe thermal comfort?
?
Thermal comfort depends on environmental factors including air temperature, thermal radiation, humidity, and air speed together with personal factors such as activity and clothing. [[02 - Sources/PDF/ashrae-standard-55/pages/Page 004|ASHRAE 55 p. 4]] Concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Thermal Comfort]].

## Card 2 - Engineering application

How should an HVAC team translate varied occupant needs into a verifiable thermal-comfort strategy?
?
**Vault-authored engineering application:** Define activities, schedules, exposures, and representative conditions; coordinate zoning, terminals, controls, and adaptive opportunities; document the selected evaluation method; and commission sensors and control responses.
