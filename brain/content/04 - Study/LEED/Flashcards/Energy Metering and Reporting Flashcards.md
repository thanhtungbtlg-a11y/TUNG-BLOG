# Energy Metering and Reporting Flashcards

Source concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Energy Metering and Reporting]]

## Card 1 - Rating-system distinction

Why must BD+C energy-metering evidence not be treated as equivalent to O+M energy-reporting evidence?
?
BD+C addresses the designed monitoring scope under the applicable energy standard together with LEED reporting provisions, while O+M requires measured whole-building energy by source and ongoing reporting. The O+M requirement does not automatically import BD+C end-use metering rules. [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 090|BD+C p. 90]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-bd-c-rating-system-november-2025-clean/pages/Page 091|BD+C p. 91]] [[02 - Sources/PDF/leed-v5-o-m-rating-system-february-2026/pages/Page 038|O+M p. 38]] Concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Energy Metering and Reporting]].

## Card 2 - Documentation recognition

Which fields should an MEP meter schedule coordinate so measured data can be traced to a reporting obligation?
?
**Vault-authored engineering application:** Record the measured quantity, boundary, device, location, units, interval, responsible party, communications path, and downstream report.
