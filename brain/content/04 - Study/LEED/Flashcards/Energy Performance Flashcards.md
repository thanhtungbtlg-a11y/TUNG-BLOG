# Energy Performance Flashcards

Source concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Energy Performance]]

## Card 1 - Evidence distinction

Why must a BD+C energy-performance pathway not be treated as O+M performance evidence?
?
BD+C evaluates design-stage energy performance through a pathway tied to the applicable energy standard, while O+M evaluates measured building energy performance over an operating period. Design compliance and measured operating results answer different evidence questions. ## Card 2 - Engineering application

How should an MEP team coordinate loads, HVAC, service water heating, pumps, controls, modeling, commissioning, and metering when pursuing energy performance?
?
**Vault-authored engineering application:** Reduce loads first, compare coordinated system options, carry selected assumptions into controls and design documents, verify operation through commissioning, and use metering to compare actual performance with intent.
