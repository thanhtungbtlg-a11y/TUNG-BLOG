# Ventilation and Indoor Air Quality Flashcards

Source concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Ventilation and Indoor Air Quality]]

## Card 1 - Rating-system distinction

What distinguishes BD+C ventilation and indoor-air-quality design from O+M ventilation verification?
?
BD+C investigates outdoor-air conditions and designs ventilation and filtration provisions, while O+M calculates required outdoor airflow, measures delivered airflow, and checks operating conditions. ## Card 2 - Engineering application

What system map should an MEP team build before finalizing ventilation controls?
?
**Vault-authored engineering application:** Map outdoor-air intakes, exhausts, contaminant sources, air-cleaning components, sensors, occupied zones, access points, and testing-and-balancing locations so control sequences can be checked against the complete air path.
