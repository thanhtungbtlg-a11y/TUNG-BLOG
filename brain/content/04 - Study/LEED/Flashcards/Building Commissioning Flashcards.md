# Building Commissioning Flashcards

Source concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Building Commissioning]]

## Card 1 - Rating-system distinction

How do the validated BD+C and O+M commissioning scopes differ?
?
BD+C commissioning verifies project systems against the owner's project requirements through design, construction, and handover; O+M applies retrocommissioning or monitoring-based commissioning to existing operations, correction, verification, and current facilities documentation. ## Card 2 - Engineering workflow

What project-stage checkpoints keep commissioning connected from predesign through operation?
?
**Vault-authored engineering application:** Use checkpoints at predesign, design, construction, handover, and operation to preserve owner requirements, verify system behavior, close issues, transfer usable records, and monitor performance.
