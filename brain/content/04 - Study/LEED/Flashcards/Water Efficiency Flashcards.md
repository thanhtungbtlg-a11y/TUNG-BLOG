# Water Efficiency Flashcards

Source concept: [[03 - Concepts/LEED/MEP/Water Efficiency]]

## Card 1 - Evidence distinction

How do the validated BD+C and O+M water-efficiency evidence scopes differ?
?
BD+C addresses design-stage metering and installed minimum or enhanced efficiency scope, while O+M uses measured whole-project water sources, operating-period performance, and subsystem trends where pursued. ## Card 2 - Engineering application

Which boundaries belong in an MEP water balance before meter locations are finalized?
?
**Vault-authored engineering application:** Separate potable and alternative sources, indoor fixtures, irrigation, domestic hot water, cooling-tower makeup and blowdown, and other process-water uses before assigning whole-project and subsystem meters.
