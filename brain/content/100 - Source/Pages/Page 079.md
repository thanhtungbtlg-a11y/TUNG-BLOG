I
STRATEGIES FOR CONSERVING MATERIALS THROUGHOUT
A PROJECT’S LIFE-CYCLE:

+ REUSE EXISTING BUILDINGS AND SALVAGED MATERIALS. Selecting resources
that have already been harvested and manufactured results in tremendous
materials savings.

+ PLAN FOR SMALLER, MORE COMPACT COMMUNITIES. Reduce the need for new
roads and other infrastructure by preventing sprawling land-use patterns.

* DESIGN SMALLER, MORE FLEXIBLE HOMES AND BUILDINGS. Use space-efficient
strategies, reduce unused space such as hallways, and provide flexible spaces that
can serve multiple functions.

* USE EFFICIENT FRAMING TECHNIQUES. Advanced framing, in which studs are
spaced 24 instead of 16 inches on center, and structural insulated panels, which
combine framing and insulation into one rigid component, use less material than
conventional framing without compromising performance.

* PROMOTE SOURCE REDUCTION IN OPERATIONS. Designate office supply
reuse centers. Encourage paper conservation through double-sided and
electronic printing.
