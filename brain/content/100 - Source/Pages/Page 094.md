RATING SYSTEM STRUCTURE
The LEED rating systems consist of prerequisites and credits. Prerequisites are required elements or green
building strategies that must be included in any LEED-certified project. Credits are optional elements—
strategies that projects can elect to pursue to gain points toward LEED certification.
