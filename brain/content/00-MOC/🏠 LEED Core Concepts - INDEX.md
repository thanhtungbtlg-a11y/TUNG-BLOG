## ⚡ Quick Reference

### LEED Certification Levels
| Level | Points |
|-------|--------|
| Certified | 40–49 |
| Silver | 50–59 |
| Gold | 60–79 |
| Platinum | 80+ |
> Total available: 110 points (100 base + 6 Innovation + 4 Regional Priority)

### LEED Professional Credentials
- **LEED Green Associate** — Kiến thức cơ bản green building, ima
- **LEED Accredited Professional (AP)** — Chuyên sâu theo rating system
- **LEED Fellow** — Lãnh đạo và đóng góp cho ngành
